## Facts (Book of the Dead; all standard Egyptology, no exotic claims)
- Weighing of the heart, spell 125: the dead's heart weighed against the feather of Ma'at in the Hall of Two Truths;
  the jackal-headed god runs the scale, the ibis-headed scribe writes it down; a heavy heart is eaten by Ammit
  ("Devourer of the Dead": crocodile head, lion/leopard front, hippo back) = the second death, gone for good.
- The heart was the seat of mind and memory (it was left in the body when mummifying; the brain was thrown away).
- 42 judges (assessor gods); the dead recite 42 denials ("negative confession"): not stolen, not lied, not killed,
  not been angry without cause, not been loud-voiced, not eavesdropped are all real items on the list.
- Heart scarab, spell 30B: a stone beetle laid on the mummy's chest, inscribed "O my heart... do not stand up as a
  witness against me". Many survive in museums (British Museum, Met, Cairo). "Everyone cheated" = Spud's joke, not a claim.
- Pass = the Field of Reeds (endless harvests).
