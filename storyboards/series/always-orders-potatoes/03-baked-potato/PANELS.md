## Departures (reasons)

- **The back wall is a painting** with the panels' look (fireplace left, beams, lanterns, the cow chalkboard, the bar
  with barrels on the right); details that differ are listed after the plate is picked.
- **Frame 0:** the Samurai's hands are coming back off the board. He stands a step up behind Viking, and a potato's
  arm can't reach the middle of the table from there (as in episode 2); the board's clack is the first sound.
- **The back row stands on a raised floor**, so the Samurai's face and hat are above Viking's hair and blossom as drawn.
- **Phoenix's counter is the end of the bar** behind Reaper; his phone goes down onto it in panel 8 ("table-edge height").
- **One spoon** for the whole meal: the first bite, the toppings and the offer (a big serving spoon). Panel 2 and 8 draw a
  fork; one held utensil keeps the hand from swapping grips, and a loaded baked potato is eaten with a spoon.
- **The last butter pat goes on with the spoon**, not two fingers: the grip library has no fingertip hold for a small
  cube, and a new hold needs Jonny's approval first.
- **Gunslinger has no utensil**: his left glove pushes the steak plate away by its rim (panel 4), his right rests.
- **The topple is two steps:** the tower leans (tweened, the cause: the top pat slides on the melting cheese), then each
  topping goes as its own rigid body, aimed onto the steak plate (physics). The bake decides where each one ends up:
  the sour cream lands on the steak, the cheese flops against it and the butter and bacon skid across the table toward
  Gunslinger, so the steak is hit rather than buried.
- **Five toppings, not a dozen**: butter, sour cream with chives, cheese, bacon, butter. The tower has to stay under
  Viking's eyes, or the spoon hand covers his face on every trip.
- **Viking tears the foil with his left glove**; the spoon stays in his right fist (a thin prop in a fist that pulls
  foil hides its handle end).
- **Panels 6-7 are a closer cut** on the steak and Gunslinger's face (the wide is back for panel 8): in the wide the
  toppings on the steak are a few pixels at phone size.
- **Panel 7: a fist of joy** instead of both hands on the board: the gloves pulling the board went into the table and a
  topping bowl.
- **The tower is smaller than panels 5-7 draw** (five toppings, not a heap): a tower as high as the panels' covers
  Viking's eyes with his spoon hand on every trip (face-covered), and the bake can only land a few pieces on the steak.
- **Panel 8's offer is with the right hand** across his chest toward Phoenix: the panel raises the spoon in his left
  glove, and moving the spoon from one glove to the other needs a hand-to-hand pass the grip library doesn't have yet.
- **Frame 0 follows reach rule B1** (7 Nov): Gunslinger, the victim, whole in the wide and already eyeing the giant
  potato beside his steak, Viking (the troublemaker) mid-shout; the board's tock and Viking's line are the first sounds.
- **Captions sit on the red cloth** under the topping bowls (y 1262), inside TikTok's safe zone and clear of every face.
- **Music**: there is no country style in the music engine; "bounce" (plucked, swung, 104 bpm, G) is the nearest, and
  adding a new style would change every other clip's seeded music.
- **The baked potato is bigger than the panels draw** (head-sized, 0.52 m long): at the panels' size it read as a flat
  grey saucer next to the steak plate at phone size, and "Look at the SIZE of it!" needs a giant (review B note 1).
- **Panel 5's "touch closer" shot is dropped**: a closer wide between the loading wide and the topple close-up made a
  jump cut (camera lint); panel 5 plays in the wide.
- **The topple lands at 7.55 s, not 7.4**: a slower, higher arc lets the bake land the sour cream and the cheese on the
  steak; faster throws skidded every piece off the plate.
- **Known glitch for the next cut:** the spoon slips in Viking's fist during the first bite (1.6-2.0 s, prop-pop) and his
  hand passes his eyes at 1.67-1.97 s; one held grip through the whole bite fixes both, and needs a new spoon hold.
