Place in every panel: a warm old English pub at daytime. Cream plaster wall over dark wooden panelling, dark timber
beams, a leaded window with small diamond panes in the middle (blue sky, old houses), a brass wall lamp left of it, a
framed landscape painting right of it, bunches of dried hops hanging at the top right. Panel 1 (wider) also shows a
Union Jack and a fireplace with a fire at the right. A dark oak table with a red tartan runner down its middle toward
us; a wooden pepper mill and a glass salt shaker front left, a white gravy boat front right.

Two rows (every panel): front row at the table, left to right Gunslinger (cowboy hat, red bandana), Viking (orange
spiky hair, fur collar, red cloak; later a white napkin bib), the Wizard (purple hat with a yellow band, purple cloak,
a staff with a purple crystal). Back row standing, higher: the Samurai as the waiter (navy, blue sash) back left,
Reaper (black hood) back right. Phoenix is not in it.

## Departures (reasons)

- **The back wall is a painting, not the panels' exact wall.** It has the warm pub look, the beams, the brass lamps and
  a big leaded window with diamond panes (drawn onto the painting), but bookshelves where the panels have a framed
  landscape, and no Union Jack or fireplace (panel 1 only, optional in the brief). The painter would not paint a close
  plaster-and-panel wall without furniture; the shelves are hidden behind the back row in most shots.
- **Dried hops** hang from a real oak beam across the top of the frame (everything that hangs is attached), not only at
  the top right.
- **Panel 1:** the Samurai's hands are coming back off the plate at frame 0; he stands a step up behind the front row and
  a potato's arm can't reach the middle of the table from there, so the plate is already down (the clack of the plate
  is the first sound).
- **The back row stands on a raised back floor (0.3 m)**, so their faces sit over the front row's heads as drawn.
- **One roast potato each** goes onto the heap in panel 4 (as drawn), not two: Gunslinger flicks his off his fork,
  Reaper flicks his from the back row (a real throw, physics).
- **Panel 8:** no hat pull. The Wizard leans back with his free glove up near his face; a glove on the brim would
  pull the crown through the head (CLAUDE.md contact rule 8).
- **Music:** there is no 6/8 jig style in the music engine yet; the clip uses the "march" style (fifths, folky) at 116
  bpm in D major.
- **Subtitles** sit low on the table (y 1330, inside TikTok's safe zone) in every shot: higher up they sat on the back
  row's faces (review A).
- **A long table** runs on toward the camera, so the bottom of the 9:16 frame is oak and tartan with the plates and the
  condiments in the corners (the panels are wider than 9:16; a short table left an empty floor there). The front row
  stands a body-width apart, so in the wides Gunslinger and the Wizard are cut by the frame's edges, as in panels 1, 4
  and 6.
- **The Wizard's staff is in his left hand** (the costume's own side), so it never stands in front of Reaper's face.
- **Gunslinger and Reaper already have a roast potato on their forks at frame 0** (they were about to eat it), so panel 4
  is one flick each instead of a stab and a flick in 0.6 s.
- **Panel 8:** Viking turns well round to the Wizard, so the fork arm reaches out in front of his chest; his fork is in
  his right hand, and a reach straight across would have covered his own eyes.
- **The napkin bib** is smaller on screen than drawn: the table and the heap cover the lower half of a potato's body.
