# Friends try to 11 "Hot air balloon": panel sheet v1 (director's panels, 18 Nov 2026)

Hook first (Jonny 18 Nov, on trial): on screen "One of them is too heavy." + caption question "Which one is too heavy?";
the ending answers it with a picture. Natural counter (no number on screen): the friends in the basket, 4 -> 3 -> 2 -> 1.
Cast references: Spud, Chip, Wedge, Mash reference sheets (references/cast/).
Jonny 18 Nov 19:57 Berlin: before Wedge says "Fine!" another friend says "Wedge, you're too fat." (Mash, eyes on his phone; panel 5). Sheet v1 was already drawing without it.

PROMPT START
One portrait storyboard sheet, 12 numbered panels in a grid of 4 columns x 3 rows, each panel a tall vertical 9:16
frame with a thin black border and a small panel number in its top-left corner. Hand-drawn anime look, warm and very
detailed backgrounds (Ghibli-like painted landscape), clean ink lines, soft cel shading. Draw the four potato friends
exactly as on the attached reference sheets: SPUD (medium round potato, teal knit sweater, one green leaf on top),
CHIP (TINY potato, half Spud's height, yellow hoodie with white drawstrings, a two-leaf sprout), WEDGE (big wide
potato, red puffer jacket with a cream fur collar, sunglasses pushed up on his head), MASH (tall thin potato, grey
hoodie, headphones round his neck, always looking at his phone). Same skin colour for all four. Setting: a grassy
hilltop at sunrise, gold sky behind, one old oak tree at the side, morning mist in the valley. A big hot air balloon
with bold warm vertical stripes (red, orange, cream; no letters, no logos) above a low wicker basket with a padded
brown leather rim, low enough that all four faces show over the rim. Four ropes tie the basket to wooden stakes in
the grass. The only words on the sheet: the short lines given in quotes below, lettered small in white with a dark
outline in the upper third of their panel, never over a face.
1. Wide, low angle: the balloon full and straining up, the burner flame roaring, but the basket still sits on the
   grass and the four ropes are pulled tight. All four friends packed shoulder to shoulder in the basket: Wedge and
   Mash at the back, Spud, and tiny Chip at the front. Chip's yellow hoodie looks suspiciously lumpy, the tip of a
   croissant peeks out under its hem. Text: "One of them is too heavy."
2. Close-up: the burner blasting a tall blue and orange flame; below, the basket's bottom pressed into the grass, a
   stake rope tight as a guitar string.
3. Medium: Spud and Wedge heave three small sandbags over the rim; one thuds into the grass.
4. Wide: still on the ground. All four look up at the balloon, puzzled.
5. Medium: Spud, Chip and Mash all turn and stare at big Wedge. Wedge is offended.
6. Wedge climbs out over the rim, one short leg over. Text: "Fine!" The basket lifts a hand's width off the grass.
7. Same angle a moment later: the basket has dropped back onto the grass. Three friends left inside.
8. Mash climbs out over the rim without looking up from his phone. Two left: Spud and tiny Chip.
9. Close two-shot: tiny Chip looks up at Spud with a sweet innocent smile. Text: "Your turn." Spud sighs.
10. Wide: Spud, Wedge and Mash stand on the grass outside; tiny Chip ALONE in the basket, the burner roaring, and the
    basket still will not lift. The three outside stare at him. Text: "Chip?"
11. Medium on Chip: he lifts the hem of his hoodie and the whole hotel breakfast buffet tumbles out over the rim
    onto the grass: croissants, a stack of pancakes with syrup, sausages, waffles, apples, a juice jug, a green melon.
    His friends' mouths fall open.
12. Wide, looking up: the balloon shoots up into the gold sunrise sky, Chip tiny and waving from the basket; below,
    Spud, Wedge and Mash stand next to a huge pile of breakfast food, looking up. Text: "It was free!"
