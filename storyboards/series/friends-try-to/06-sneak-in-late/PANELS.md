## Departures (with reasons)

- **A 3D room, not a layered painted environment.** A night interior is the hardest light for the env painter and the
  brief allows the fallback; every panel's camera works in the 3D hall.
- **The door opens inward against the left wall** and is closed by Viking from inside; the leaf swings shut along the wall.
- **The cheer is a diagonal seen from the right** (panel 6 has them bunched face-on): the hall is 2.8 m wide and four
  bodies with their arms don't fit abreast without going into each other or hiding a face.
- **Viking stands behind the Wizard for the lift**, his gloves on the Wizard's flanks, the Wizard's back to him: the
  only hold where no glove crosses a face or a body (brief: "both arms round his sides, no intersection").
- **The coat stand tips toward us**, away from the Wizard; Phoenix catches it from the front.
- **No snore**: there is no snore sound in the whitelist yet (a new sound needs measuring and describing first).
- **The ending glarers stand in the near foreground at the far end of the hall from Viking**, with the camera high, so
  Viking's face shows above their heads (panel 8 has them closer and bigger; the vertical frame can't hold that and
  Viking too).
- **Mom's door is face-on at the end of the hall, right of centre** (the brief has it on the right wall): edge-on
  from the near end its light never read at phone size. Its warm wedge of light and her shadow now run the length of
  the hall to the glarers' feet.
- **The rusty hinges are big but not in close view**: the brief wants them close from 0.00, the panels put the open
  leaf at the left edge; a camera beside the jamb would need a restage of 01-06. The hinges were made larger instead.
- **Reaper is behind Phoenix at frame 0** (panel 1 shows his eyes): every spot in the 2.8 m hall where his face shows
  puts him in the lift's path or under Phoenix's crest. He shows from 0.3 s.
