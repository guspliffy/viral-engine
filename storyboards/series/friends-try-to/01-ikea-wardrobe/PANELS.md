Departures, with reasons:
- Two rows instead of one line: the potatoes are 0.85 m tall and the frame is 9:16, so the wide shot is higher and
  further back than the panels' eye level to hold all four faces (Viking back-left above Phoenix, the Wizard right
  above Reaper).
- The wardrobe is 1.4 m (72 % of a real one) so it reads as tall next to the potatoes without leaving the frame.
- Panel 7 has Viking lean on its left side and it folds flat backwards (doors up) to land on panel 8's picture; the
  board's tip to the right is kept as the first rack (bend) before the fold.
- Panel 5: Reaper turns the key at the wardrobe's front right corner (not in front of it), so the front camera shows
  the whole wardrobe squaring up past him; the brief's close insert of the key (6.15-6.55) carries the quarter turn.
  Afterwards he side-steps right with the key, and the Wizard waits behind on the right by the window, so the wide
  ending has all four faces with the wardrobe's column clear.
- Panel 2: the wardrobe hangs high in the swirl with its doors half open from the top (a cabinet with a thin plinth
  doesn't read "legs up" on its own).
- Foreground clutter: an opened box front left and a spare shelf across the front, as in the panels.
- No burned-in title (Jonny types it in-app), so the top of frame 0 stays clear.
- Music: the brief asks for a Nordic folk fiddle; beat.mjs has no folk style, so the plucky "bounce" style.
