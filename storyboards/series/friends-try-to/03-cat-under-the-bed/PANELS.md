Departures, with reasons:
- Lying flat on the floor (Wizard, Phoenix in panels 1-4) is a new movement for the rig; CLAUDE.md asks for a natural-
  motion study before one is built, so they crouch low and lean in instead.
- Panels 1-4 use a higher camera than the panels' eye level so that the back row's faces (Viking, Reaper) clear the
  front row's heads in a 9:16 frame (the potatoes stand; in the panels the front two lie down).
- Behind the bed the order is Viking, Wizard, Phoenix (the panels: Wizard, Viking, Phoenix): they scramble round the
  bed's foot end after the hiss, and Viking, who is already there from the lift, goes first and furthest so nobody runs
  through anybody. They get there in the scramble (the brief's "visible reason"), not in a walk at 5.5.
- The empty box stands in the room from frame 0 (front left, among the clutter); Reaper sits down beside it instead of
  carrying it in. Carrying it from the door would cross the scramble.
- The room is a painted plate (assets/plates/cat-bedroom.png): its window is back centre and the glass door back left,
  so the door with the carrier is off the plate; the carrier stands back right in 3D.
- No cat voice is in the sound whitelist: the hiss is a short air hiss, the "mrrp" is left out, the sneeze is Viking's
  gibberish "ah-CHOO" with an air hiss under it.
- No burned-in title (Jonny types it in-app), so the top of frame 0 stays clear.
- Panels 5-8 (LOW) sit higher than the panels' floor-level camera (2.5 m up, looking down onto the bed) and Reaper sits
  close to the lens, low in frame: from floor level his hood rose over the back row's eyes.
- Reaper keeps a deadpan nod in the cheer (panel 6) instead of a raised fist: his fist would cover the back row.
- The four stand at least 1 m apart (their arms and costumes are wider than the bodies), so the back row spreads
  a little wider than in the panels.
