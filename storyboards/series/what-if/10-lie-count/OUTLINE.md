# There's a catch EP10: "What if you could see how many times everyone has lied to you?"

The 8-minute cheap-film TRIAL (Jonny 18 Nov 2026, "lets test it over night"): wide 16:9 YouTube film, built with one
kit made once, shot types computed from each place, the narrator carrying most of the time. The story is the data in
`film.mjs` (SCENES); this file is the readable version. Opener A (the count runs at frame 0).

**Hook (0:00):** his best friend eats Spud's cinnamon bun, says "Delicious!", the number over his head ticks 211 -> 212.
On screen "212". Narrator: "What if you could see how many times everyone has lied to you? Your best friend just hit
two hundred and twelve." Caption question: "Why has his best friend lied to him 212 times?"
**Ending that releases it:** every lie was for him (a year of Fridays learning to bake his birthday cake).

**Cheap-method rules used:** the Happiness 03 town (terrace, counter, street, river, bridge, rain, room, balcony, hill)
reused as built; seven shot types (wide, two, group, close, over-the-shoulder, alone, insert) computed from a place's
stage, with a slow push or pull on every shot; character lines are captioned gibberish; one film score from library
takes (`score.py`); no new design, no hand or foot polish, no holds (props sit on tables).
No character names on screen (relations only). Real sounds only.
