## Story chain

- No rings (box opens empty) -> viewer sees the empty slot + Mash walking far away + 20 -> 01 wide, 02 insert.
- So he jumps; he touched the bow tie last -> bow tie hangs, drops into Chip's glove -> 03, 04 same wide.
- He touched the rings last -> rings stay, spin on the hallway floor -> 05, 06.
- Box arrives empty -> Chip opens it, looks at us: "...Again." -> 07, 08; Mash halfway.
- He clutched the box -> box lands in Chip's other glove -> 09; Mash a few rows away.
- He works out the rule (rings in glove, looks at hand, at cactus) -> 10; narrator states the rule.
- Decoy = cactus -> two flinches, counter pulses -> 11.
- He scratches his leaf -> leaf stays, drifts to the shelf -> 12.
- Rings arrive, Spud is bald -> Chip's look up, Spud pats head, guest gasp -> 13.
- Rings go on -> 14; flash freezes the photo with everything Chip holds + bald Spud -> 15.
- Payoff tag: leaf beside cactus under the old photo with the leaf -> 16, "Would you still jump?"

## Departures (and why)

- Sides: Chip LEFT, Spud RIGHT in every altar shot (board swaps in 04/07/09/13): one screen direction so the viewer
  never re-finds the two, and Chip's growing pile of things stays in one place.
- Two arches: the board's arch at the far end is the ENTRANCE arch Mash walks through; a matching ALTAR arch stands
  behind Chip and Spud (behind the camera in the wides, seen in 15). Keeps the board's look and the wedding's logic.
- 12: Spud is in frame scratching his leaf when he goes (the brief's beat); the board shows only the ring.
- 02/08/14 background blur on the board: drawn sharp (house rule: whole frame sharp).
