## The world and the one rule
- One place: a four-storey brick apartment block at night, clear cold sky (deep blue-purple), a street lamp, a pavement in front. Chip's flat is on the floor above the street (his face stays big on a phone). The front door has a **red fire alarm box** on the wall right beside it (pull lever, small glass panel). Inside: a lobby with a big round **lobby clock** (no numerals) over the stairs.
- Each rewind drops Spud back on the pavement in front of the door, one minute before the fire reaches Chip.
- **The catch, the only rule: every rewind, the fire starts earlier.** The lobby clock and the fire's size show it, no cards.
- Spud: teal knit sweater, one leaf. Chip: smallest, yellow hoodie, two leaves. Neighbours: generic potatoes in pyjamas/bathrobes, one grumpy old neighbour (bathrobe, hair curlers).

## Rules check
- Stake + clock in the first 2.4 s, same words in text, voice and in-app title line 1 ("Your best friend. One minute."). Opener B named.
- Cause and effect: loop 1 fails → rewind → loop 2 fire earlier (catch) → loop 3 even earlier, nobody listens, he falls, watch cracks → "can't outrun it" → he stops running to Chip and pulls the alarm the moment he lands → everyone out before the fire shows → they think he's crazy → the smoke proves him right.
- Never two voices at once; captions upper third; no time-jump cards; fire never touches Chip; no burns, screams or injury; no news framing, no emergency-alert tones.
- Dramatic recorded-sounding orchestra from 0.0 (time-travel score recipe, assets/music/time-travel/README.md), ~0.45 under the voice; realistic sounds only, no whoosh on the rewind.
