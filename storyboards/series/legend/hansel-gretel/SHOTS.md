CAPTIONS: UPPER THIRD (centre y ~580, below the banner which ends at y 504), never over a face or eyes (move per shot if needed), normal size (~74 px Inter 900, max ~900 px), 2-4 word phrases, key words red (#ff3b3b) matching /gingerbread|smell|turn around|red eyes|bone|oven|hungry/i. Music: Sol brief /mnt/project-files/videos/folklore-legend/hansel-gretel/sol-music/answer-summary.md, beds assets/music/ai/hg-* (open, forest, sweet, hunger, oven, run). Dropouts as listed there (red eyes ~200 ms, before "Stick out your finger" through "Still thin", the shove through one beat after the slam, before "Turn around"). Voice x1.15 already applied; never trim a take except inside real silence.
