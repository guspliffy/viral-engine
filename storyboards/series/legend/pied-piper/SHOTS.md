# The Pied Piper of Hamelin: shot list (H1 whistle + E1 kid pays back)

Two worlds. TODAY = the hosts on Bungelosenstrasse, Hamelin, at dusk (warm amber lamps, half-timbered houses,
carved beam with an illegible carved inscription, cobbles). 1284 = the legend, drawn in the jewel colours and dark
lead lines of the lost Marktkirche window (c. 1300): flat glass panes, leading between colour areas, slight glass
mottling, light shining THROUGH colour. Transition today -> 1284: the candle glow fills the frame and turns into glass.
Candle = fear meter (tall warm when safe, low and blue near the threat). Shots change every 1-3 s, camera always moves
(push, pan, tilt); dialogue shots close enough that faces read on a phone. No lettering burned in; subtitles stay.
Children's fate non-graphic: fog and a closing hill, never a body, never fear on a child's face held long.
