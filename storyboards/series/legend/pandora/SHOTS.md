Subtitles UPPER third (centre y ~580, never over faces or eyes). Banner (legend-pandora-title.mjs, Jonny's pick) above the subtitles, gone by ~2.6 s. Two exports: pandora-draftN.mp4 WITH subtitles (Reels/YT) and pandora-draftN-nosubs.mp4 (TikTok, he turns on TikTok captions).
Sound: the SILENCE is the motif. Music from 0.0 except the frame-0 hush (room tone + one low string), hard silence on L17 when the lid lifts, music returns warm on L21 (Hope).
