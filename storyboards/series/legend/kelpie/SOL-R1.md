## Part B — Glitches and logic

1. **0.00–1.20 s, 1.80–3.67 s.** The black foreleg and its reflection merge into the bank and water. The giveaway fold in the opening kneel is hard to distinguish at phone size.
2. **3.67–7.66 s.** The three children register as color blocks in the establishing wide. Their faces and reactions are too small to read.
3. **7.66–8.91 s.** The horse's torso is visible behind the children, but the right edge cuts off its head. The next angle suddenly supplies the whole animal.
4. **12.37–14.88 s.** The warning angle cuts the horse's body at the left edge and leaves a sliver of the tan basket hanging in the lower right without a visible carrier.
5. **~13.8–14.8 s.** "One finger" crosses the mane and muzzle, masking the horse's facial detail and bridle hardware as it turns into view.
6. **14.88–16.17 s.** The dare pose draws a long pointing arm out near the boy's cheek while both resting hands are still visible. His fingertip meets or goes out of the right edge near the end.
7. **16.17–20.62 s.** The blonde rider is aboard in the first kneel frame, before "The first one climbed on." The second and third appear already seated at the 18.94 and 19.97 cuts. Their straight legs and overlapping silhouettes make the mounts look like figures placed on top of the horse.
8. **20.62–23.44 s.** The cut swaps the lowered horse for an already raised, elongated pose before the growth phrase. The foreground and riders shift sideways together, making the stretch read mostly as a reframe. The pan crowds and clips the muzzle at the right edge around 21.4–22 s.
9. **23.44–25.22 s.** The reaction wide squeezes the host toward the right edge while most of the horse is outside the left edge. The kid's face is too small for his disbelief to carry the shot.
10. **25.22–28.46 s.** Grandma's face is small in the first warning setup. The second crop cuts the staff lantern against the right edge, hiding part of the prop that carries her fear cue.
11. **29.86–34.66 s.** The setup cuts away the kelpie's hindquarters and any view of the riders. The horse settles nearly face-on to camera while the kid stays screen-right, weakening the eyeline promised by "turned its wet face to him."
12. **~34.4–37.97 s.** The visible gap on the approach jumps to a nearly joined pose across the 34.66 cut. The next insert starts with the finger already laid over the muzzle outline and a rounded pink sleeve floating in open water-colored space. The exact point where skin meets nose is ambiguous.
13. **37.97–42.65 s.** The reaction shows the kid's extended hand in open space, and the lore wide shows the horse's nose with the kid missing. His finger appears free well ahead of the release at ~70.8.
14. **38.90–44.90 s.** Dark coats and the black horse blend in the wide framings. Rider faces are tiny, and the hands are unreadable even when a rider says her hands are stuck.
15. **42.65–44.90 s.** Only the blonde rider remains on a back that visibly has room behind her. The brown- and green-clad riders reappear at the 44.90 cut without a dismount or return.

17. **48.32–50.38 s.** The close angle darkens the kid below a horizontal water overlay that crosses his lower face and the horse's muzzle. The following knees shot jumps him back to a shallower apparent depth.
18. **50.38–52.80 s.** Ripples run high across the pink coat on "knees," and translucent legs show through the water in both setups. The knee-to-chest step relies mostly on tint and is hard to locate against his body.

20. **54.89–60.91 s.** The underwater crops remove the back of the horse and push the host's feet against the right edge, with a shoe clipping there by about 59.8. The dive wide and the next pose also jump from the kid trailing at arm's length to his body pressed in against the cheek.
