# Skip the boring parts (happiness EP02, outline v1, 16 Nov 2026)

Life question (never answered directly): "Does skipping the boring parts make you happy?"
Title a stranger can picture: "He skipped every boring part of his life."
Narrator speaks to the viewer as "you" (Jonny 16 Nov); Spud is the one on screen. Opener A (the remote is already in his hand at frame 0). Target ~45-50 s. Narrator voice 1 on nearly every beat, Spud/Chip gibberish captioned, captions upper third.
On screen the whole video: an AGE counter (top), jumping with every skip. Reads on mute.

Shoulder tap (Lana K save, via the ideas thread): the hook makes them stop, the next line tells the viewer why it is about them. It also opens a loop the rewind closes.

Cause and effect: each skip causes the next age jump; the empty old age causes the rewind; the rewind shows exactly the moments skipped in beats 0-2; the ending pays off the joke Chip starts at frame 0.

Comment question options (Jonny's idea, a test):
- A (recommended): "Would you press it?" with on-screen "YES or NO", no comment instruction. Easy to answer, people argue both ways.
- B: "Does skipping make you happy? Comment yes or no."

Music (from 0.0, under the voice ~0.45): light quick pizzicato for the skip montage, drops to solo piano at 82, warm orchestra from the rewind to the sunset. Sounds: real remote click, rain, traffic; no whooshes.
