# Happiness film "I could see everyone's happiness score" (outline v2, Jonny OK 16 Nov 2026)

Source of truth: Claude Doc https://claude.ai/artifact/NN6YVvLJRoz7SiD8LeeaNJ (this file is its copy for builders).
WIDE 16:9 YouTube film ~3:20 in four ~50 s blocks, each also cut as a tall 9:16 part with its own hook.
Opener A. Narrator (voice 1 "deep storyteller") speaks to the viewer as "you" on nearly every scene, never over a
character line; Spud is on screen. Life question (never answered out loud): can you find happiness by looking for it?
No contest: Spud wants to LEARN Mash's secret. Clock = Mash moves away, train Sunday at sunset (HUD countdown).
Twists: 0:45 every check = -1 / 1:30 Mash's number falls when Spud isn't with him / 2:10 Mash copied Spud / 3:06 the
90 only the viewer sees. Real facts (verify before the build): Schooler et al. 2003 music study; Killingsworth &
Gilbert 2010.

tune (C), strings building to the tune in full at the colour flood (D). Realistic sounds only (no chime on -1).
