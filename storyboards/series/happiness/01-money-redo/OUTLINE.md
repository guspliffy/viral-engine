Why it is different from drafts 1-5: the old story was a heist and then fear; Spud mostly reacts and his friend is on screen for 2 s.
Here there is ONE goal with a clock and a real chance to fail, Chip is beside him the whole way (viewers attach to him), and the ending is Spud's own choice that costs him the ten million.
No crime, so he stays likeable. The "115 days" fact is the cause of the next beat, not a lesson.

Music (from 0.0, under the voice ~0.45): ticking pizzicato + orchestra for the spree, building with the counter; solo piano when Chip walks off; low tense strings for the last minute; full warm orchestra on the hill.
