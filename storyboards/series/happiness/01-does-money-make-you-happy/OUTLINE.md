# Does money make you happy? (pilot, outline v1, 14 Nov 2026)

New series: Spud looks for happiness. Narrator (voice 1 deep storyteller) leads, Spud speaks gibberish with captions.
Opener B (cold open). Target 40-45 s. Hook reads without English.

## Stake-first opening (0.0-2.5 s)
- Picture: grey evening, rain on the windscreen of a gold sports car on a coast road. Spud in fur coat, gold chain, sunglasses pushed up; money bags piled on the back seat, one split open. His face is empty.
- Narrator: "He has ten million. He hasn't smiled in thirty days."
- Text, upper third: "💰 = 😊 ?"

## The chain (each scene caused by the one before)
1. "Thirty days earlier." City street at noon. Spud in his teal sweater, empty pockets. Rich characters everywhere look happy (limousine laughter, shopping bags, a yacht poster). Spud: "Mo-ney... ha-ppy?" -> so he makes a plan. (small high)
2. HEIST: ski mask over the sweater, vault door swings, bags; alarm, getaway through the city, police (dark caps, no real markings) lose him. He laughs. (big high, action)
3. SPENDING, each step bigger: the gold car, the coat, the mansion. Narrator: "He bought everything happy people have." (highest high)
4. TURN: first night in the mansion, a siren passes. He freezes, hides the bags. Narrator: "But now he had something to lose." Music turns.
5. FEAR CLIMBS: bags in the fridge, under the floor; one lock, then ten; a knock at the door, he doesn't open (it was Chip). Can't sleep, 3:00 on the clock.
6. LOW: alone at a long table in the huge dark hall, one candle, eating in silence.
7. A siren STOPS at his gate. He grabs the bags and flees in the car = the opening shot.
8. The car runs dry on the coast road. He climbs the hill with one bag, looking back for police.
9. FAKE-OUT: lights and siren below... an ambulance passes. Nobody was ever coming.
10. PAYOFF: the clouds break, a huge sunset over the sea. He sits, the bag falls open, the wind takes a few bills; he doesn't reach for them. First smile.
    Narrator: "For one moment, he forgot everything he owned."
    Hold on the sunset (the most love in the whole video), real ending, no title card.

## Highs and lows
city (up) -> heist (up) -> spending (top) -> siren (drop) -> fear climbs (down) -> alone (bottom) -> flight (tension) -> ambulance (release) -> sunset (highest).

## Music (from 0.0, under the voice ~0.45)
