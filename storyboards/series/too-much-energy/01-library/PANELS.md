## Departures (with reasons)

- **The bookcases stand in a staggered diagonal row across the back** (each turned 30 degrees), not side by side along
  the right. The panels show them falling back and diagonally and leaning on each other; in a 9:16 frame a row that
  recedes to the right is the only way all five fit and still fall onto each other.
- **The Sorcerer stands on a low dais behind his desk.** In 9:16 his face would sit behind the readers' heads; old
  libraries raise the issue desk, so his face shows between theirs.
- **Panel 07 is shot from the front**, not from behind Phoenix (the brief's note: one camera side for the series).
- **The three don't line up in the rubble for the shush**: Reaper and the Wizard stay behind the table and the
  Sorcerer on his dais; they turn to Phoenix together. Walking them into a row would cost a second of the ending and
  put the Sorcerer behind Phoenix.
- **The ladder is on the left** (the front bookcase), not back right as in panel 01, so the ride runs left to right
  toward the end stop and the fall carries on to the right across the frame.
