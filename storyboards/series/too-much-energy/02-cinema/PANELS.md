# Too Much Energy 02: the cinema (Jonny's eight panels, 5 Nov 2026)

Board: `/mnt/project-files/video-scripts/2026-11-05-too-much-energy/storyboards/02-cinema/storyboard-eight-panels.png`
(4x2 grid). The panels are the target; the brief (`02-cinema-ENGINE-PROMPT.md`) sets the mechanics.

Every panel is shot from the screen, looking back at the seats, slightly below the front row's eye level. The room is a
dark red cinema: red velvet seats in rising tiers, warm wall sconces (two each side), the projector window glowing white
at the top centre with a beam, the faces lit cool blue-white from the screen in front of them. The front row is close,
the seat backs and armrests cut by the bottom of the frame.

## Departures (with reasons)

- The set is a painted back wall (the far tiers, the projector window, the sconces) behind three rows of real 3D seats,
  not a layered env: everything the cast touches is a seat, so the seats are geometry.
- The Dragon's giant soda and the Astronaut's small popcorn stand in the armrest cup holders: their hands stay free for
  the shield and the lift, and nothing passes through a held cup.
- Panel 04's catapult close-up is a short cut in from the same side, then back to the wide for the bucket's flight.
