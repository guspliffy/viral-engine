# Kid vs Dad 05 CARROTS HELP YOU SEE IN THE DARK  (house at night, spy mission)

Label at 0 s: "When your dad says carrots help you see in the dark".
Sent to: whoever made you eat your carrots. Stake: the cookie jar Dad hid on the top shelf; Tato can only get it
in the dark. Natural counter: Tato goes a little more orange with each plate of carrots.
Hidden twist, teased 3 times: the cookies disappear every night (crunch from the kitchen at night / crumbs in Dad's
moustache at breakfast / the jar lighter every day). Dad is the one who goes in the dark.

Hook options
- **H1 ★** Dad pushes a plate of carrots across the table. DAD: "CARROTS. Eat them, and you see in the dark."
  Cut to green night-vision: the REPORTER in the dark hallway: "In the dark. Is it true?"
- H2 Dark first: black screen, two orange glowing eyes open. REPORTER (night vision): "DARK. Total darkness. And
  this kid can see." Hard cut back to dinner: Dad with the carrots.
- H3 Mission first: the cookie jar on the top shelf, a red laser line. REPORTER: "MISSION. Get the cookies. In
  the dark."
