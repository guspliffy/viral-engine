# Kid vs Dad 04 DAD JOKES AT THE WAITER  (restaurant, daytime)

Label at 0 s: "When your dad makes jokes at the waiter". News bar: "LIVE: WAITER IN DANGER".
Sent to: your dad, or the friend who works as a waiter. Natural counter: Tato sinks lower under the table each joke.
Why the rule exists (teased): a framed photo of Dad on the kitchen wall, "DO NOT SERVE".

Hook options
- **H1 ★** The waiter (Mash, apron, notepad) walks up, sweating. REPORTER (behind a menu at the next table):
  "DANGER. A dad is about to order." Tato shakes his head at Dad. REPORTER: "Will the waiter survive?"
- H2 Joke first: WAITER: "Can I take your order?" DAD: "Sure. Where are you taking it?" Rimshot, Tato's face
  hits the table. REPORTER: "DANGER. Will the waiter survive?"
- H3 Proof first: close-up of the "DO NOT SERVE" photo. REPORTER: "BANNED. This man was banned. Why?"

Ending options
- **E1 ★** The waiter puts the bill down and walks away fast. Push-in on the bill: "TIP: STOP." CUT.
- E2 WAITER: "Enjoy your meal." Tato pops up: "You too." He freezes. Dad, slowly, a proud tear. CUT.
  (Everyone has done this; Tato turns into Dad.)
- E3 The waiter fires back: "Steak's on the house." / Dad: "On the house? Is it a roof steak?" / WAITER: "No. A
  missed steak." Dad goes silent for the first time. TATO: "Finally." CUT.
- E4 Dad: "Can we get the bill?" / WAITER: "Sure. Bill, come here." A potato named Bill walks in. Dad, proud, to Tato:
  "He's good." CUT.
