# Kid vs Dad 06 SWALLOWED GUM GLUES YOUR STOMACH  (kitchen, bomb squad)

Label at 0 s: "When your dad says swallowed gum glues your stomach".
Sent to: your dad or mum (everyone heard it; in German "Kaugummi verklebt den Magen").
Clock: a kitchen egg timer ticking. Natural counter: Dad's bomb-squad gear grows (oven mitts -> colander helmet ->
full laundry-basket armour). Hidden twist teased 3 times: Dad never chews gum, gets weird about it, his own tummy
gurgles. Why the rule exists: Dad's X-ray.

Hook options
- **H1 ★** Frame 0: a gum bubble pops, Tato gulps. DAD (frozen in the doorway): "SWALLOWED. Don't. Move." The
  REPORTER in his helmet, crouched behind the fridge: "Swallowed. Is it deadly?"
- H2 Countdown first: an egg timer ticking. REPORTER: "DANGER. Seven years left." Pull out: Tato on the kitchen
  table, Dad in oven mitts.
- H3 X-ray first: a glowing X-ray of a potato with a big ball inside. REPORTER: "STUCK. Since 1995. Whose is it?"

Ending options

  DAD (sweating): "...That's different." CUT.

- E3 Tato hiccups, a pink bubble floats out of his mouth and pops on Dad's face. Dad faints. CUT.
- E4 Dad's tummy gurgles loudly, a muffled "pop". DAD: "...Mine just came unstuck." CUT.
