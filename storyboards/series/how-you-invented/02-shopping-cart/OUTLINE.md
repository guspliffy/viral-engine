**Motive**
- M1* Mash's rule "Drop it, and it's MINE." Your arms are full, so everything you drop is his.

- H3 Cold fail: you scream down the hill on a basket with wheels, snacks flying. NARR: "Day three of your war with Mash."

**Ending** (last line keeps "You've invented the shopping cart", then the flip)
