## The rule

Whoever holds the coal at zero, fries. Said once by the commentator at 0.0, proved by Viking by 1.5 s.

## The chain (each step caused by the one before)

1. Viking holds the coal when the clock hits zero, so the clown pulls the lever and he drops into the fryer.
2. A player is gone, so the clown sends in a fresh coal and the clock starts at 5.
3. It burns, so Spud throws it on; so does Dragon; so does Gunslinger. The clock runs down to 2.
4. It is back with Spud at 2, so Dragon and Gunslinger hide their gloves behind their backs.
5. Nobody will catch it, so Spud throws it straight up. The clock ticks to 1 while it hangs.
6. It has to come down, so Gunslinger catches it by reflex, the clock hits zero, he drops.
7. A player is gone, so the clown already holds out the next coal and the clock jumps back to 5.

Fake-out before the payoff: the coal in the air (did he beat the clock?). Stakes by 1.5 s: Viking's drop and the fire.

Jonny on the rough draft (11 Nov 2026 21:41 Berlin): "make the clown laugh evil the rest is good to go". The laugh
is panel 03 (0.75 s longer for it, so panels 04 to 12 start 0.75 s later than the sheet's plan), a short one after
Gunslinger's drop and a low chuckle as the last sound.
