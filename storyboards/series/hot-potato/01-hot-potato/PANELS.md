# Hot Potato pilot: panel notes (Jonny's sheet, 11 Nov 2026)

Sheet: `hot-potato-sheet-2026-11-11.png` (10 panels, made by Jonny from `../01-hot-potato-IMAGE-PROMPT.md`).
The sheet is the target for staging, camera, look and detail. It wins over the outline and the prompt text.
Status: waits for Jonny's go. No build before that.

## Set (same in every panel, camera always from the front)

- Back wall: blue curtain folds with cotton-wool clouds left and right, pastel bunting along the top
  (pink, yellow, teal, purple flags).
- Smiling yellow cardboard sun top centre, closed happy eyes, blush. A number card hangs under it on two
  rings (bold brown digit on cream). Panel 08 shows the sun WITHOUT the card (slow motion inside second 1).
- Rainbow arch behind the players with a wooden-look sign HOT POTATO in brown letters, one yellow star at
  each end.
- Stage: a row of four square tiles with a white stitched (dashed) border each, on a wooden stage front with
  posts. Tile order left to right: pink (Viking), mint (Spud), yellow (Dragon), lilac (host).
- Under and in front of the stage: one huge steel fryer vat, bubbling golden oil with sparkle, thin steam,
  a wire fry basket hanging at its right side. Visible in the lower third of every wide panel.
- Light: bright soft pastel from above, warm orange from the oil below.
- Red tile rule: the tile under the coal holder is red (01 Viking, 03 Spud, 05 Dragon, 06 Spud, 07 Dragon,
  08 host). While the coal is in the air no tile is red (04).

## Characters as drawn

- Spud: dark teal knit sweater with rolled collar and cuffs, brown boots, white gloves, one green leaf sprout.
  A little smaller than Dragon.
- Dragon: orange-brown wings, small horns, row of spikes on his head, leaf sprout, no clothes.
- Host (Wizard): purple robe and pointed hat with yellow stars, big rainbow bow tie, white gloves.
- Viking: orange hair and beard, fur collar, red tunic, dark gloves and boots.

## Three places where the sheet needs a decision for the build (my reading, told to Jonny)

1. Tile colours drift between panels on the sheet. Build: fixed pink, mint, yellow, lilac; red only under
   the coal holder.
2. The host's hat flies off in 08 and 09 and is back on in 10. Build: the hat drops into the hole after him
   (small beat with a plop), so he comes up wearing it.
3. In 03 the host stands next to Spud although Dragon stands between them. Build: the host steps along the
   front of the stage for the handover, then goes back to his lilac tile before the clock reaches 3.

## Not in the panels

- No potato is shown in the oil: 02 and 09 stop at the hole, the splash and sizzle are sound only.
- The on-screen countdown runs 5 to 0 once. HOT POTATO is the set's sign, no title card.
