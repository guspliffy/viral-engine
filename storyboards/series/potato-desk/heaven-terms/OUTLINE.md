## The idea in one line
He clicks "I agree" on heaven's terms without reading. Every clause comes true. The last one sends him to hell.

## Natural counter (no number on screen)
The scroll. At frame 0 it drops and unrolls down all 100 steps, from the gate to hell's hatch at the bottom: clause n lies on step n.
Each clause the Angel quotes is a step lower. Clause 100, the last, lies on the hatch. The viewer sees the end coming from frame 0.

## Jonny's 5-step pattern
1. Power word, conflict, foreshadows the end: "EVERYTHING. Forever." + the scroll rolling down to hell's red glow.
2. Question, 3 words: Spud: "Who reads these?"
3. Rule: Angel: "At heaven's gate, there's one rule: you agree to everything. Forever."
4. Story escalates the rule: each clause worse (harp at 6 AM, wet clouds, your ex next door, no wifi); the Angel turns up deadpan every time: "Clause 22. You agreed."
5. Fast end: clause 100 read on the hatch, the hatch drops, CUT within ~0.6 s.
