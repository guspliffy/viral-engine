# Potato Desk: dialogue sketch test (brief, 17 Nov 2026)

Jonny, 17 Nov 11:39 UTC: "lets try this, we could even create characters for the new scenes."
Source: analytics/2026-11-17/saved-videos-outlier-study-2026-11-17.md (/mnt/project-files/saved-videos-outlier-study-2026-11-17.md).

## Why

In the 17 animation accounts Jonny saved, almost every outlier is two characters talking in plain English about an everyday unfairness, in a nearly still picture. Outliers vs the same accounts' normal posts: 10.3 vs 4.6 shares and 60 vs 44 likes per 1,000 views. We use gibberish or a narrator and get about 11 to 15 likes per 1,000. This test asks one question: **does spoken two-character dialogue lift our likes per 1,000?**

## Format (fixed for every episode)

- **Two characters, one still wide two-shot** across a desk or counter, for the whole clip. No camera moves. At most one cut-in for the final punchline, and only if it's needed.
- **45 to 70 s.** Lip-flap, blinks, small gestures and one prop gag are enough. No action scenes.
- **The first spoken line states the conflict, with a number, by 0 to 3 s.**
- **Three to four escalating beats, then a twist line at the end.** The last frame may loop to the first.
- **Plain English voices, one fixed voice per character** (voice source: Jonny's choice, see the open question at the end).
- **Captions:** one line low in the centre, bold, with one key word coloured. A fixed title box at the top for the whole clip, in the same words as the TikTok title.
- **Sound:** a quiet music bed under the voices, plus one sound effect per beat.
- **Cast rule:** potato characters only (Jonny, 8 Nov). A fighter sits on one side. On the other side sits a new potato with a boring job (Jonny wants new characters for these scenes). New designs go to a Fable 5.2 design subagent per the model split.

New potato characters needed (designs, not decided yet):
1. **Desk Clerk**: tired service-desk potato with a lanyard, a headset and a "NEXT" ticket machine. Used in episodes 1, 3 and 4.
2. **HR Manager**: neat potato with glasses, a clipboard and a fake smile. Used in episode 2.

Sets: one service counter (customer service, bank and registry are the same counter with a different sign) and one small office with a desk. Two sets cover all four episodes.

## Posting and measurement

- Post pack per the reach rules: TikTok title with the text tool. Caption line 1 is the situation, line 2 asks which one they are. Tags are exactly #funnyanimation #animation plus one situation tag (#customerservice, #performancereview, #bank, #paperwork).
- Name the series **Potato Desk** and put it in its own playlist.
- **Main measure: likes per 1,000 views at 24 h** (Jonny's base signal). Also logged: shares per 1,000, 0:03 hold and finish rate.
- **Pass mark:** the median of the first 4 episodes reaches 30+ likes per 1,000 (about double our current level), or shares reach 3+ per 1,000. A single post above 7,500 views (10x our median) counts as a breakout, and the breakout rule applies (3 follow-ups in 48 h).
- This tests a format, not the look. The stickman question waits until this result is in.

## Episode 1: Reaper's Help Desk (about 55 s)

Set: service counter, sign "AFTERLIFE CUSTOMER SERVICE". Reaper behind the counter; Chip walks up as the customer.

- CHIP: Hi. I died three times today and I'd like to speak to a manager.
- REAPER: I am the manager.
- CHIP: Then I'd like to speak to your manager.
- REAPER: (points up) He's... busy.
- CHIP: The first time, I slipped on a fry.
- REAPER: (typing) Slipped... on... fry.
- CHIP: The second time, the same fry.
- REAPER: You didn't pick it up?
- CHIP: I was dead!
- REAPER: And the third?
- CHIP: (quiet) I went back for the fry.
- REAPER: (long stare) Okay. I can offer you Premium Afterlife. Nine ninety-nine a month. No fries.
- CHIP: Is there a free version?
- REAPER: Yes. (stamps a ticket) Your waiting time is four hundred years. Please hold.
- (Hold music. Chip sits in a chair. Cut back to the same wide shot: Chip is a skeleton still holding the ticket. Reaper:) NEXT.

Title: When customer service is the afterlife
Caption line 2: Which one are you, the fry guy or the "please hold" guy?

## Episode 2: Viking's Performance Review (about 60 s)

Set: small office, HR Manager behind the desk, Viking squeezed into a tiny chair.

- HR: Ragnar. Your yearly review. Teamwork: two out of ten.
- VIKING: Two?! I carried the whole team. On my shoulders. Into battle.
- HR: That was the Christmas party.
- VIKING: It was a battle.
- HR: You carried Steve from accounting through a window.
- VIKING: He was slow.
- HR: Communication: one out of ten. You answer every email with "AAAAARGH".
- VIKING: It saves time.
- HR: Punctuality: zero. You arrive by boat.
- VIKING: The parking is full!
- VIKING: What did I score on raiding?
- HR: We don't have a raiding category.
- VIKING: (stands up slowly) You do now.
- (Same wide shot, a second later: the office is empty and the supply closet door swings open. Viking walks past carrying every chair, the printer and Steve.)
- HR: (writes on the clipboard) ...Initiative. Ten out of ten.

Title: My performance review went well
Caption line 2: Which one are you, the HR form or the "AAAAARGH" email?

## Episode 3: Dragon at the Bank (about 50 s)

Set: the same counter, sign "BANK". The Clerk is behind it; Dragon leans over it, too big for the frame.

- DRAGON: I'd like a loan. One thousand gold coins.
- CLERK: Do you have any savings?
- DRAGON: One million gold coins.
- CLERK: (long pause) ...Then why do you need a loan?
- DRAGON: I can't spend my savings. That's my bed.
- CLERK: Your... bed.
- DRAGON: You don't sleep on your money?
- CLERK: I sleep on a mattress.
- DRAGON: (horrified) Poor thing.
- CLERK: I need a photo for your ID. Please smile.
- (Dragon smiles. The flash goes off and Dragon sneezes fire. Same wide shot: the clerk is black with soot, hair smoking.)
- CLERK: (calm) Approved.

Title: When a rich friend asks to borrow money
Caption line 2: Which one are you, the dragon or the mattress?

## Episode 4: Phoenix at the Registry Office (about 50 s)

Set: the same counter, sign "REGISTRY OFFICE". The Clerk is behind it; Phoenix fills out a form at the counter.

- PHOENIX: Hi. I'd like to report a death. Mine.
- CLERK: (not looking up) Again? That's the fifth time this month.
- PHOENIX: I'm a phoenix. It's kind of my thing.
- CLERK: I need a death certificate and a birth certificate.
- PHOENIX: Same day?
- CLERK: Same day. Form 7B. Both sides.
- (Phoenix writes. Halfway down the page Phoenix starts to glow.)
- PHOENIX: Oh no. Not now. Not now...
- (Phoenix bursts into flames. The form burns to ash. A baby phoenix chick sits in the ash.)
- CHICK: (tiny voice) Hi. I'd like to report a birth.
- CLERK: (slides over a new form) Form 7B. Both sides.

Title: Me doing paperwork at the registry office
Caption line 2: Which one are you, the one who loses the form or the one who says "both sides"?
