Opener B (cold open). Cast: Spud (Fight, survives), Chip (Freeze), Wedge (Flight). ~30-32 s. Narrator voice 1.
Music: dramatic orchestra from 0.0 (low strings + heartbeat, brass/timpani on the attacks), turns hard and heroic at

1. COLD OPEN 0-1.5 s: thunderstorm night, lightning shows a 400 kg black bear on its hind legs over the tent, claws
   ripping the canvas, three potatoes inside. Cut back. Narrator: "You're camping in the wild."
2. Spud's phone: "No service". Through the tent mesh they watch the bear smash the windscreen and tear the car door
   off; the car alarm screams and the headlights flash across the camp. "A bear is in your camp. Fight. Flight. Or
   freeze. Only one of them survives."
3. The bear knocks the gas lantern over; the dry grass starts to burn. It comes into the tent. Chip plays dead in his
   sleeping bag; the bear grabs the bag and drags him out into the dark. "Freeze lasted ten seconds."
4. Wedge bolts out past the fire and runs; the running sets off the chase, so the bear drops the bag and goes after
   him. He climbs a pine; lightning shows the bear climbing right after him, branches snapping. Cut on the treetop.
   "Flight lasted thirty seconds. Black bears climb."
5. The bear comes down and turns to Spud. He grabs a burning branch, stands tall on the cooler, bangs the pot and
   roars. The bear charges full speed and stops one metre in front of him, stands up and roars in his face. He
   doesn't step back: he keeps roaring and banging the pot against the burning branch right in front of it. He
   never hits it. The bear drops to all fours, turns and runs into the forest.
6. Sunrise, smoke, rain stopped: Spud alone with the bent pot; an empty torn sleeping bag at the forest edge, claw
   marks up the empty pine (implied, nothing shown). "You can't beat a black bear. But you can scare it. Never run. Never play dead."
   "Which one are you?"
