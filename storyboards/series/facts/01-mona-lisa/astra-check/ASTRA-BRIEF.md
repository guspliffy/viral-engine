## House rules (keep them)

- True story only; never "nobody knew her" (critics did), say "nobody stopped for her".
- No character names on screen or in lines (the thief stays "this man"/"he").
- The hook uses only common knowledge (it must work for a stranger with zero context) and opens ONE gap that the
  ending releases. The first spoken line names the stake and the clock.
- No title card mid-video; no call to action; realistic sounds only; music under the narrator.
- Cheap for us to change: narrator lines and their order, cuts and camera angles, which shot comes first, captions,
  the on-screen text, music hits, trimming scenes. Expensive: new sets, new characters (each ~1 extra hour).
