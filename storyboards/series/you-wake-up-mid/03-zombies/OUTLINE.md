## Real moves (story world logic, zombie advice is fiction)

Shoot only what's in reach; noise draws them; close and block the door; go up, stairs make them come one at a time;
always keep a second way out; a gap jump needs a run-up (the mistake). Gap ~2 m between roofs of the same height.
