## Story chain (X, so Y, so Z)

- Spud wakes at the wheel as the car flies off the broken bridge (POV, Mash screaming) -> so the car hits the lake (03, spray).
- It floats, water hisses in (04) -> so: belt off, a clock starts at 1:00 (you have about a minute).
- Mash grabs his phone to call (05) -> so Spud knocks it down: the call costs the minute ("No phone.").
- Mash tries the door instead (06) -> it won't move: the water outside holds it ("Not the door.").
- So Spud tries the window (07) -> it moves 2 cm, then the electrics die ("Window. Now.").
- So he rips out the headrest and breaks the side window with its prongs (08) -> the lake pours in.
- The water rises to Chip's chest on the back seat (09) -> so Spud gets the small one out first ("Small ones first.").
- The car tilts nose down (10) -> so Spud follows Chip out; Mash is still at his door, so Spud drags him out ("Leave it!").
- The car sinks away below them (11) -> so they swim up ("Up.") and burst through the surface.
- They crawl onto the shore, alive (12) -> "One minute. That is all you get." -> his eyes close, and open in a smoky
  bedroom with the alarm going ("You wake up mid house fire.") = the loop.

## Departures (and why)

- 02: sprout whipped flat (outline), not upright (panel).
- 06: Mash's face turned 3/4 to us (the wrong move must read on a face).
- 09: water to Chip's chest (the rising water is the clock the viewer sees).
- 12: Spud on his back, eyes closing (the loop needs his eyes).
