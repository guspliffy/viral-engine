## Fact check (two sources each, done 12 Nov)
- About one minute: a car floats roughly 30-120 s; the expert number is "about 60 seconds" to get the window open
  (CBS / Giesbrecht, KUOW, Royal Life Saving WA, NPR 2024). Narrator says "One minute".
- Seatbelts, Windows, Out, Children first (Giesbrecht, Univ. of Manitoba; CBS SF; Kids and Cars).
- No phone call first: it costs the seconds you need (Giesbrecht in IAED Journal, CBS Philadelphia, HeraldNet).
- Not the door: water pressure holds it shut; waiting for the car to fill is a myth (NPR/WHYY 2024, Royal Life Saving WA).
- Power windows can die within seconds; break the SIDE window (corner), never the windscreen (Royal Life Saving WA,
  Art of Manliness). Headrest prongs as the tool: common advice, weaker source; the panel can show a window hammer
  instead if Jonny prefers.
