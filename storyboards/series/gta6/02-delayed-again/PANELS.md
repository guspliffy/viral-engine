  only a strip of it.
- **The calendar's grid is 7 × 5**: the panels draw a smaller symbolic grid. A real month reads as a calendar at
  phone size, and the cell after the circle is "the day after launch".
- **Phoenix jumps between places at the cuts** (beside the sofa in 1–6 and 8, under the calendar in 7), as the panels
  place him. He never flies across the room on screen: that path went through the Wizard.
- **No TV in frame, no blue TV light**: the panels show the TV's back at the bottom of the frame. In the engine it read
  as a black block over the table, so it is off camera, and with it went its blue glow on the faces. The room is lit by
  the warm lamp and the dusk window.
