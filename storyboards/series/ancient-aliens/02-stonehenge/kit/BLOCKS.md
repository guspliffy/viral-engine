## What this series lives off (Jonny 14 Nov): "images with love to the detail and nice to look at... + good interesting commentary + music supporting the scenes"

Three things, in this order, outrank speed. (1) **Every shot is a picture worth looking at on its own:** composed, rich
in drawn detail (frost, lichen, tool marks, long shadows, the skyline, crows, the sun), lit by the low sun; before an
animatic goes out, look at one still per shot and send back any that looks plain or empty. (2) **Commentary that is
interesting:** STORY.md's lines, each over the picture that proves it. (3) **Music that supports the scene:** the score
changes with every chapter; time your cuts and landings to it.

## Rules every block keeps (so the join is seamless)

- Branch off `aa-02-stonehenge-kit`. Your files: `storyboards/series/ancient-aliens/02-stonehenge/block-<a|b|c|d>/` only.
  **Never edit a kit file** (`engine/page/props-sh-*.js`, `props-aa-*.js`, `kit/`, `../../kit/`): if a piece is missing
  or wrong, message the kit thread ("Make ancient aliens Stonehenge episode") with the kind and what is needed.
- **Two frames, one generator** (`sh-kit.mjs` header): build(F) for `FRAME.wide` and `FRAME.tall`, ids
  `aa02-<block>-wide` / `-tall`. The WIDE storyboard is the master: gates, animatic and Jonny's judgement happen on it.
  The tall one is made AFTER his OK on the wide animatic: same timing and sound, its own cameras (closer, or a pan
  where the wide frame holds two things side by side) and caption spots (F.SUB, F.NUM). Do not render the tall cut
  before that OK. Wide establishing shots must be RICH: frost, long shadows, the skyline, crows, the sun; never an
  empty half of the frame.
- **The sun is the clock.** Give every place the story time T of its shot (`today(id, o, { T })`): sky, ground and
  stones then agree. The sun comes down from the upper LEFT of the axis view to the tallest stone. The countdown
  `sunsetClock(c, F, T0, t0, t1)` runs in every shot from 0:20 to 3:03 except full-frame inserts where it would sit
  on the picture. One storyboard per light (`SET_OF(T)`); past scenes use the light of the today scene they cut from.
- Sheet misses NOT to copy: 06 (the sun comes from the LEFT of the tallest stone, not the right). Where a sheet and
  BOARD differ, BOARD wins. Today's tallest stone (56) stands ALONE with one knob; the pair is whole only in 2500 BC.
- Host: Spud (`potatobrawl/spud`) with the headlamp band in every scene (`host()`), lamp off (it is day). He holds
  nothing: he points, knocks, or lays a palm flat on stone (the palm on a stone is the cut to 2500 BC, same frame).
  Reaper's voice: every line with `hostSay(c, F, t, who, line, dur)` (never `c.say`). Lines are STORY.md's, word for
  word. Never two voices at once. Time everything after a line from the spoken length the render prints.
- **Places and the checks.** One place per origin `PLACE(i)` (2000 m apart, never closer: a plain's far skirt reaches
  1100 m). The kit's land and stones need NO check window: the gate judges them by their real surface, so a body that
  goes through a stone fails as "shell" and must be moved, not waived. Hat and scarf are see-through for the checks.
  The only standing window is `allowClip: lampClip([...host ids], END)`; a crew member ON the parked ship takes one
  window with `r<id>-ship` and a reason. A prop in the air on purpose carries `staged` (the kit's ships and lifted
  stones already do). `bench/make-sh-proof.mjs` passes preflight in both frames: copy from it (map cameras
  `MAP_VIEW.south` / `.north` in wide, `.all` in tall; the plant shot = `ringShip(id, o, { hover: 16 })`).
- Each block starts and ends on a hard cut at its story time; keep its length within 1 s of its slot. First and last
  0.3 s: no line running, no move half done. Each block's first 2.5 s must work alone as a TikTok opening.
- Captions in the upper third, never over eyes or faces. `clueTag(c, F, t0, t1, n)` in the block where the clue
  starts. Most viewers are German speakers: picture and numbers tell the clue without the English.
- Sound: `music: score(T0)` in every storyboard, animatics included (the join lays the same file once). The score is
  SILENT on purpose at 0:49.6-0:54, 1:40-1:42, 2:57-3:00 and under "It's a parking space.": put the bell knock, the
  chewing, the wind and the dry line there. Realistic sounds only: stone thud and clack, frosty grass steps, sheep,
  crows, far wind at about 0.06, a low physical hum for the ships; no whooshes, chimes or blings.
- Project rules as always: cause and effect, clean never-glitchy picture, hands checked, preflight / clear / layout
  gates, lean (weekly limit: no extra rounds), the animatic goes to Jonny before polish, one full render per frame
  shape, merge only on his "merge it".
