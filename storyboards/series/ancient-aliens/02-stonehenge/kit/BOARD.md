## 1. Axes and the sun

The monument's axis is the world's z axis. **+z = north-east** (entrance, Heel Stone, Avenue, midsummer sunrise; the
default camera stands here and looks down -z), **-z = south-west** (the tallest pair, the midwinter sunset),
**+x = north-west (screen right from the default camera), -x = south-east (screen left)**, up +y. The centre of the
stone circle is the origin of a place.

The sun is the film's clock. It comes down from the upper LEFT of the default view (the south) to the axis. `az` is
degrees left of the axis (toward -x) as seen from the centre, `el` degrees above the horizon:

| Block | Story time | Light (one storyboard per light) | Sun at start -> end (az, el) | Sky |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Today the sun at (0, 0.3) touches the LEFT edge of Stone 56 (the lone tallest stone stands just right of the axis). In
2500 BC it sits in the slit of the tallest pair. Past scenes use the same sun as the today scene they cut from.

## 3. Sizes (real numbers; Spud is 0.88 tall)

- **Sarsen circle:** 30 uprights on a centre-line radius of 15.5 (ring 31 across; the film says "thirty metres"). Each
  upright 2.1 wide x 1.1 thick x 4.1 above ground, gaps 1.1, faces flat, sides gently swelling. Stone n (1..30, clockwise
  seen from above, Stone 1 is the first one south-east of the entrance): angle a = 6 + 12 (n - 1) degrees from +z toward
  -x, at x = -15.5 sin a, z = 15.5 cos a, facing the centre. The axis passes between 30 and 1 and between 15 and 16.
- **Top stones (lintels):** 30, each 3.25 long x 1.0 wide x 0.8 high, curved to the ring, ring top at 4.9. Every upright
  has TWO round knobs (tenons, r 0.16, 0.16 high: drawn stronger so they read) on its top; every top stone two round holes underneath and a
  vertical ridge (one end) and groove (other end) that lock it to its neighbours.
- **Five giant pairs (horseshoe, open to +z),** each pair two uprights 2.4 wide x 1.2 thick with a 0.45 slit and one
  top stone 4.8 x 1.3 x 1.0, facing the centre. Centres (x, z), height of the uprights above ground:
  51-52 at (-5.6, 4.0) 5.0; 53-54 at (-6.3, -2.2) 5.5; **55-56 at (0, -6.3) 6.7** (the tallest pair, slit 0.5, on the
  axis; 2.4 more underground: the film says "nine metres long"); 57-58 at (6.3, -2.2) 5.5; 59-60 at (5.6, 4.0) 5.0.
- **Bluestones (about 80):** an outer ring of 60 rough natural pillars at radius 11.8 (0.9 wide x 0.5 thick x 1.6-2.0
  high, hash per stone) and an inner oval of 19 dressed pillars (0.6 x 0.6 x 2.0-2.4) at radius 5.4 inside the
  horseshoe, open to +z.
- **Altar Stone:** 4.9 (x) x 1.0 x 0.5, lying flat across the axis at (0, -4.2).
- **Heel Stone:** rough unshaped boulder 2.4 wide x 4.7 high, leaning 20 degrees toward the circle, at (1.6, 77).
- **Earthwork:** bank crest at radius 46 (2500 BC: 1.2 high, chalk-white with grass creeping in), ditch at radius 52
  (6 wide, 1.4 deep), entrance gap 11 wide on +z. Today: a soft grassy dip and rise, 0.4.
- **Avenue:** two banks 21.5 apart (x = +-10.75), from the entrance straight along +z to the horizon.
- **Grooves (clue 4):** inside the Avenue, two parallel lines at x = +-3 from z = 60 outward. Today: two low grass
  ridges 2.5 wide x 0.35 high. 2500 BC: two fresh chalk grooves 0.6 wide x 0.3 deep.
- **Today's ruin** (same positions): circle uprights standing: 1-7, 10, 11 (half width, 2.4 high), 16, 21, 22, 23,
  27-30; lying where they fell: 8, 9, 12, 14, 15, 19, 25, 26; gone: 13, 17, 18, 20, 24. Top stones in place: over
  30-1, 1-2, 2-3, 4-5, 6-7, 21-22 (six). Giant pairs complete: 51-52, 53-54, 57-58. **Stone 56 stands alone**
  (one knob on its top, leaning 3 degrees); 55 lies broken in two toward +z across the Altar Stone with the pair's
  top stone beside it; 60 stands alone, 59 lies in three pieces. Bluestones: about 20 of the ring as stumps and
  pillars, 6 of the inner pillars standing. Slaughter Stone lying flat at (0, 44), 6.4 x 2.1.
- **The slope (panels 29-30 only):** the real ground falls about 0.5 across the circle while the ring's top is level
  to a few centimetres. For that one place the kinds take `slope` 1.2 (the fall from -x to +x across the circle,
  uprights longer on the low side, ring top level): drawn 2.4 times too strong so it reads on a phone.
- **Ring ship on the ring (climax):** the pilot's `ringship` at s 1.03 (r 15.5) settles with its hull's underside at
  y 4.9. Past scenes of the build: it hovers 40 above its work, lift beam r 1.4 (a stone is 2.1 wide).
- Everything is REAL scale: the earthwork is 110 across and the camera sees 300, so no miniature. The far layer
  (plain, skyline) is a dome on the camera as in the pilot.
