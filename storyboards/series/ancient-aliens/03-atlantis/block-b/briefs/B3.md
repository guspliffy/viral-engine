THE METHOD (it works, R2 proved it): each painting is projected from its own camera onto real 3D surfaces at their real
depths (sky/clouds/far hill on a far shell, the temple hill and town on cards or rough masses at their distance, walls on
their planes, water on y 0), so a camera that pushes or side-steps a few metres sees parallax. Everything a character
stands on, touches or that is within ~8 m of the camera is REAL modelled 3D in the painting's colours (crops of the plate
may be its texture), with contact shadows (a ShadowMaterial plane over the walkable top at +0.004 is fine), so Spud and the
crew sit IN the scene. Light: noon (sun az 10 el 65, BOARD); a real-3D surface must match the painting's value where it
meets the projection.
