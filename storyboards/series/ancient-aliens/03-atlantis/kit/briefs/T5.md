CONTRACT (export AT_TOWNS_KINDS, AT_TOWNS_GRIPS = {}):
- envflash   spec.env (env name), spec.mask (png in that env's folder, white = metal that flashes; made by flashmask.py
             from the env's paint.png: hue/sat/value thresholds + a hand polygon option), spec.layer (which layer card it
             rides on, or depth), spec.color (flash `#ffd66a`, hot `#f08a2e`), spec.dir (screen direction of the run).
             Param flash 0..1: a soft-edged band 1.5 plates wide sweeps across the masked metal (additive, never a
             sprite), then param hot 0..1 leaves every 4th plate warm (plate grid from spec.plate [w, h] in px of the
             painting, or found from the mask). It must stay locked to the painted plates when the camera moves inside
             the env's safe range.
- maproute   on a flat map plate: spec.from / spec.to / spec.via in plate pixels; an ink-red line `#8f2a1f` drawn on
             by param draw 0..1 with a slight hand wobble, dots `#d02a20` that pop in (param dots), two small inked stone
             pillars at a given pixel (spec.pillars), all drawn to look like ink on that paper (no glow, slight bleed).
- mapoutline the Atlantis island outline (3,000 x 3,800 km, spec.box in plate pixels): drawn on in the line's ink in 45
             frames by param draw, then filled at 60 % strength by param fill (paler than real land: Plato's claim),
             with relief ticks inside like the map's own land.
- pagemarks  on the 1940 page plate: typed "1940" in the date line and "68" and "69" in lines 12-13 (typewriter face,
             uneven weight, ink `#3a342c`, at spec positions in plate pixels, matching the painted type size), then red
             pencil `#c8322a` double underlines drawn on by param draw (one after the other).
