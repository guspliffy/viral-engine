## Story chain

01-02 a block lands every two minutes -> the beam, the landing, the thud -> close on the slot.
03 so the site grows to 2.3 million blocks -> the counter over the whole site -> wide from behind him.
04-05 "they tell you humans built this" -> the same frame snaps to today -> he never moves, the world does.
06 so: five clues, answered when three stars rise tonight -> the three pyramids at noon -> wide.
07-08 clue 1: blocks heavier than a car -> block, Spud, car side by side -> his palm on the stone.
09 the touch takes him back -> the same block lands on the same spot at 0:00 -> same frame.
10-11 the mountain they made -> 146 m up the face, tallest for 3,800 years -> from below, then from above.
12 but the heaviest stones are inside -> the lamp clicks on -> he walks into the dark.
