- Every panel +2.75 s: Jonny's spoken title (9 Nov 14:17) comes before the promise line; 01's subtitle "Firewood till
  spring!" lands at 2.75 s instead of frame 0, with the title's subtitle on frame 0.
- 08 ends on the narrator's "Day one." (the brief says no line) and the HUD's DAY 100 flips to DAY 1 on the word (the
  panel shows DAY 100): the callback to the spoken title's 100 days, so the joke also lands on mute; it lands on the
  second blink, nothing after it.
- 01 (sheet): Viking's axe is missing from his back and 08 has no snow on his nose: the brief keeps both (the other
  thread's check of the sheet against the prompt).
- 01: the hook's subtitles sit at y 385 (the free band over the snowed window, under the HUD), not at the panel's very
  bottom: the cast fill the lower two thirds and the app's caption and buttons sit there.
- 01: the Samurai chops side-on (yaw 80, 3/4 to us), so the katana rises and falls toward the frame's middle; turned
  more to us the blade left the frame's left edge.
- From 03 on the Samurai is `duo/samurai` (katana put away): the costume katana can't leave the katana cast's glove,
  and both gloves carry the armful and rip the planks. The cut to 03 hides the swap.
- 04: no chairs and no run to the window: the window's plank is up on the cut and Reaper stays at the door (from the
  panel's close camera a run to the window and a flying chair hid faces, and the beat is 3.5 s); no breath clouds (no
