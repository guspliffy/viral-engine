## What the panels stand on

1. Spud (teal sweater, one leaf) is in every panel and nearest the camera or centre in most.
2. One place: the lift car. The right wall is the button wall, and from P05 it burns behind the cast.
3. Every panel is caused by the one before (the chain below). Never a card, never a jump to a new place without its cause.
4. Faces carry it: panic, eyes huge, mouths open from P03 on; P07 is Spud's face alone.
5. The five bake darker from P05 to P12.
6. HUD chips top corners: DAY, battery (the panels also show CABLE: departure, see below).
7. One subtitle per panel, never over a face.
8. P10 and P12 are outside: the car small in a big place (shaft pit, space).
