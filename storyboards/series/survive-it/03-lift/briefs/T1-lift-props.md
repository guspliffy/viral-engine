CONTRACT (world metres; the elevator set's car: walls x = +-2.05, back wall z = -1.6, front z = 1.25, ceiling y = 3.4, floor y = 0; the camera usually stands at about [0, 1, 7.4] looking -z, or inside the car looking at the right wall):
1. `liftpanel`: the button wall. Origin: bottom centre of the plate; with rot 0 the plate faces +z (the director turns it to face -x on the right wall at x 2.03). spec.w 2.6, spec.h 3.2. A brass plate with 20 columns x 50 rows of round buttons (number 1 bottom left, row by row, 1000 top right), each about 3.6 cm with a darker rim, instanced. spec.close [dx, y] (default [1.05, 0.72]: near the door end at hand height): a 16 cm round door-close button (two arrows pointing at each other) on its own small plate. State: `lit` 0..1000 (that many buttons, in order, glow gold; unlit ones are dull brass), `hot` 0..1 (the plate goes from brass to white-hot with thin orange cracks), `dead` 0..1 (after an explosion: plate scorched black, ragged, most buttons gone, a few ember-red dots), `punch` 0..1 (0 the close button whole; 1 the button gone and a ragged 30 cm hole with bent brass petals, dark inside; steps at 0.5).
2. `liftcable`: origin where the bundle meets the car roof (director puts it at [0, 3.4, -0.2]). A steel anchor block 0.5 x 0.25 x 0.5 on the roof and a bundle of 12 strands (each 4.5 cm thick, bundle about 22 cm across, slight twist) running straight up spec.len 8 m. State: `strands` 12..0 (the whole part = intact strands; a snapped strand shows as a 0.5 m curl at the anchor and a 1.2 m curled end hanging from above, each with its own seeded curl), `whip` 0..1 (the newest snapped ends sprung apart: 1 just snapped, wide; 0 settled), `glow` 0..1 (the intact strands glow orange-hot).
3. `liftspring`: origin bottom centre. A red coil spring, 5 turns, radius spec.r 1.3, wire 0.16 thick, free height spec.h 2.4, a dark steel disc on top (radius r + 0.2, 0.12 thick) and a base disc. State: `squash` 0..1 (1: 0.35 m high, coils stacked, radius bulges 8 %).
4. `liftshell`: the car seen from outside, built round the set's car (origin = the set's origin; put at x 0, y 0, z 0). Outer skin 8 cm outside the walls, back, floor and ceiling (steel panels, rivets, a roof beam), and at the front a 0.25 m thick steel rim round the whole 4.1 x 3.4 m opening (NO front wall: the cast inside must show), with a floor-sign box in the middle of the top rim (dark glass 0.7 x 0.26 m, green digits spec.sign, default "9999", readable at 360 px) and the two steel door panels (0.62 x 2.05 m each) thrown open and bent, hanging outward at the left and right rim. State: `scorch` 0..1 (soot and burn streaks), `count` (when set, the sign shows its whole part instead of spec.sign).
5. `shaftpit`: origin: the pit floor's centre. A dark brick lift shaft seen from the front: back wall at z -2.6, side walls at x +-3.3, a concrete floor, two steel guide rails up the back wall; walls spec.h 40 m tall (the director slides the prop in y to make the shaft rush past, so the bricks need visible variety: darker and lighter bricks, stains, a few pipes and numbered floor marks every 4 m). A riveted metal plate with "0" (0.9 m, readable at 360 px) on the back wall at spec.signY 5.2.
6. `shaftpass`: what shows through the open doorway from inside while the car shoots up. Origin: centre bottom of a flat brick wall facing -z (toward a camera inside the car; the director puts it at z 1.9). spec.w 3.4, spec.h 60. spec.floors [{ y, label }] (default [{ y: 30, label: "800" }]): at each, a door-sized (1.3 x 2.1 m) opening with a warm, bright hotel hallway painted in it (carpet, a wall lamp, a brass plaque with the label, readable at 360 px). State `blur` 0..1 (1: the bricks are drawn as vertical streaks, for when the director slides it past at speed; the hallway opening stretches a little too).
Style: hand-drawn anime, like the set: flat colours with painted variation, no perfectly regular repeated texture (hash every brick, rivet and button a little), `userData.noOutline` on glow and flat backdrop parts.
