1. `wallfire`: flames on a wall. Origin bottom centre; with rot 0 it faces +z (the director turns it to face -x at x 1.95). spec.w 2.6, spec.h 3.2, spec.seed. From t0: a white-yellow core flash (2 frames), an orange fireball swelling to 3 m across in 0.25 s with a ragged cel edge, breaking into dark smoke puffs that thin out by t0 + 1.0; spec.buttons 140 small brass discs (3.6 cm) shot out of a spec.w 2.6 x spec.h 3.2 area at 6-14 m/s in a cone, falling (g 9.81), bouncing once off the far wall (spec.far 4.0 m away) and the floor, at rest by t0 + 1.6 and staying. 3. `fireblobs`: zero-gravity fire. Origin: the box's centre. spec.n 9 round wobbling blobs of cel flame, 0.15-0.4 m, drifting slowly (0.1-0.3 m/s, seeded) inside spec.box [3.4, 2.4, 2.2], each with a soft glow. State `show` 0..1 (they swell in), `clock`.
4. `sparkrain`: sparks streaming past. Origin: the box's centre. spec.n 220 short orange-white streaks in spec.box [w 5.2, h 7, d 0.6], moving at spec.speed 9 m/s along spec.dir (-1 down, +1 up), a few bouncing sideways; State `rate` 0..1 (how many are alive), `clock`.
5. `cardark`: the lights are out. Origin = the set's origin. Dark see-through sheets 1 cm inside the car's two side walls, back wall, floor and ceiling (sizes above), covering the lit ceiling panel too. State `dark` 0..1 (opacity up to 0.62, a warm-black #140a08). It must not darken the cast (it is behind and around them, never between them and a camera inside the car or in front of it).
6. `alarmlamp`: a red alarm lamp. Origin: its base (the director hangs it under the ceiling at a back corner). A 16 cm red dome on a steel base with a soft red glow sprite; State `on` 0..1; while on it pulses twice a second from the clock and throws a faint red wash sprite (1.5 m) on what is near it.
7. `spacesky`: space, as a painted backdrop card facing +z, origin at its centre. spec.w 70, spec.h 110 (canvas 2048 x 3072, painted with brush-like strokes, not a smooth computer gradient): deep blue-black with stars of different sizes and warmth, a few four-point glints, a faint milky band; in the bottom 30 % the blue curve of the Earth with white cloud swirls, a thin bright rim of air along the curve, and at spec.hole [0.5, 0.12] (u, v from the bottom left) a small dark hole punched in the clouds with a thin grey smoke trail rising straight up from it to the middle of the card (where the lift car will be). State `glow` 0..1 brightens it a little.
