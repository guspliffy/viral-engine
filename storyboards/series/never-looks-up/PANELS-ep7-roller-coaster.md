## Departures (and why)

- Captions: the board's comic bubbles become our kinetic subtitles at the same spots (Jonny's TikTok title sits at the top).
- The place: no single painting can hold a camera that rides a loop, so the set is a sunny grass hilltop (set type
  "hilltop": the ground runs off a cliff edge to the sea side) under a painted sky dome, and each outside shot
  (01, 04, 05) has its own painted far plate of the seaside fair, sea and lighthouse cliff, made from that shot's eye.
- The track runs straight (drop, airtime hill, vertical loop, run-out past the photo camera, brakes at the kiosk)
  so the ride's real speed fits the 10 s of the board: nothing is slowed down except the flash freeze in 07.
- The Wizard has no beard: the game's Wizard model has none, and a new costume part is Jonny's call. His hat comes
  off on the drop and his sprout shows from 02 on (no white hair tuft for the same reason).
- The Dragon's puffed cheeks: the face rig has no cheek control, so "cheeks full" is a closed, smiling, chewing mouth.
- Living worlds: the hilltop isn't a painted env set, so `life: "auto"` has nothing to move; the pennants wave, the
  gulls circle and the wind sound follows the ride instead.
- 08: the kiosk screen shows a still drawn from panel 06's frame (7.97 s), so the photo really is that moment.
- 05: the car is really upside down at the loop's top, so seen from in front of it Phoenix is on the right of the
  frame and the Wizard on the left (the board draws 05 as the upright car flipped top to bottom, which keeps Phoenix
  on the left). A real roll would need the track to twist; the riders' places stay true to the car instead.
- The back row sits 58 cm higher than the front (the board's stadium seating), or the front riders hide the back
  faces from every camera in front of the car (at 42 cm Phoenix's crest still hid the Reaper's face in 01, 03 and 06).
- 06: the photo camera hangs at face height on a 3.1 m post on Phoenix's side, so the ride photo shows all four faces
  over the nose (the board's camera is lower; from there the car's nose hides the front riders' gloves and phone).
- 01: the camera rides with the car over the crest (a crane at a fixed angle to it, 6 m out and 31 degrees up), so all
  four faces stay framed while the nose tips; a fixed camera lost Phoenix by 0.4 s as the car pitched toward it.
- 05 -> 06 cut at 6.3 s, not 7.0: after the loop's top the camera in front of it sees only the car's floor, so the photo
  camera's shot starts earlier and pans with the car to the photo moment (7.3-8.0 s).
- 06: the "SMILE!" sign stands tall (its board 2.4 m up) at the lower left of the photo camera's view: a low sign there
  would stand on the track, as the camera looks across it.
- The seats have no headrests: a headrest on the front seats hid the back riders' faces in 03 and 06.
- 03/04: no white lattice towers yet (the supports are red posts under the track); a new structure is one for the next
  pass if you want it.
- 04: the camera stands 50 degrees round from side-on, on the entry side, so the whole loop fits the narrow frame as
  the board's tall oval; the car climbs its far half in the middle of the frame (the board has it on the right
  edge). The sea and lighthouse sit behind the loop's left side, the Ferris wheel in its middle; the hilltop's grass
  in front hides the painting's lower part, so the fair's tents don't show as they do on the board.
- 05: the camera hangs 4 m under the upside-down car and looks up at it, so the hanging heads sit on the sky with the
  Ferris wheel far below (level with it, the hilltop's grass filled the lower half of the frame).
- 07: the phone sinks a hand's width as his head comes up, so his small "o" shows over it.
- 08: a slow push in on the kiosk (0.8 s); the photo is cropped in on the four riders so their faces read on the screen.
- 03: the ride cam sits a little higher than the board's level camera (1.75 m over the front seats, not 1.45): from
