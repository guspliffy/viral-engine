## Departures (with reasons)

- **The zombies are our cast with grey-green skin and droopy lids only** (characters/zombie/*-zombie.json, like
  Then I'm a Zombie Too's Phoenix): ragged costumes would be new art, which is Jonny's call. No weapons.
- **Reaper shushes the Wizard with a mitten at his own mouth** instead of clamping the Wizard's: a glove on another
  character's face fails the contact check, and a clamp the check could pass would read as a slap.
- **Gibberish gets captions** (Jonny, 4 Nov): "ZOMBIES!!", "psst! OVER HERE!", "SHHH!" in the sky clear of faces and
  below the top 20 % kept for his title. Phoenix's "uhhhhh" and the groans have none (sounds, as the brief says).
- **"huh." sits over Phoenix's head** instead of the lower third: in this framing the lower third is the cast, and a
  caption over a character fails the check (the same move as episode 1).
