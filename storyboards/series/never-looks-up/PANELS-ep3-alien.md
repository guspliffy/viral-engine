## Departures (with reasons)

- **Tug of war (03):** the panel has Reaper hugging the Wizard round the waist from behind. Every version of that hold
  pushed the two bodies 50-98 % into each other (the contact gate fails it, and it reads as one blob), so it is a
  hand-to-hand chain: Reaper holds the Wizard's back glove in both of his and leans back. Same idea, clean contact.
- **The pile (04, 08):** the panel heaps the three friends on top of each other. Lying bodies merge in the engine, so
  they sit down hard a body-width apart instead, and the chair lands on the lawn beside them, not across them.
- **The Astronaut's side (01-04):** the panels put him back right. In a tall frame the back right is behind the seated
  friends, which hid his face whenever he acted, so he and the telescope stand back left, clear of the beam.
- **The alien (05-07):** the panel has it leaning out of a hatch in the saucer's side. Here the hatch is in the flat
  underside and it rides a lift disc down on a warm light column, which keeps the hatch, the gift and Phoenix in one
  frame at the LOOK-UP.
- **The held note (08.00-09.15):** the music style has no held theremin note; the beam's hum carries on as the held
  tone, then the music drops back in on the ping.
- **Gibberish gets captions** (Jonny, 4 Nov), placed in the sky clear of faces and below the top 20 % kept free for
  his in-app title.
