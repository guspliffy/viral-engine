## Departures (with reasons)

- **The water is the painting's own water floor** with 3D foam rings (the wake, the ring's ripple, the Wizard's
  splashes), splash drops and bubbles on top, not a separate water mesh: the env system has no water surface yet, and
  the painted water holds still under every shot. Everything below y 0 is hidden by it (the Wizard waist-deep, the
  shark under water).
- **Reaper keeps the megaphone? No: the Wizard keeps it.** The brief has Reaper take it back; panels 05-08 show the
  Wizard holding it, so the panels win.
- **Reaper stands on the platform** behind the front rail instead of sitting: it reads the same at that size and keeps
  both friends' faces above the rail.
- **The megaphone and whistle are held with plain grabs** (no grip in the library covers them yet); Jonny to check the
  hold on the cut.
- **Gibberish gets captions** (Jonny, 4 Nov): "SHARK!!", "LOOK UP!!" and the shark's hopeful "hiii?", in the sky
  clear of faces and below the top 20 % kept for his title.
- **The ladder climb** uses the engine's hand-over-hand `climb` (four pulls, head leading, body rising on each pull),
  not a new move.
