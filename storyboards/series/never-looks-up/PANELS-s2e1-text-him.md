## Story chain

1. The promise: the Wizard says "Watch. He'll look up." -> his face and the megaphone at Phoenix's ear -> shot 01, frame 0.
2. HEY!!! with a feedback squeal -> the bell aimed at Phoenix's ear, his crest flapping, his eyes and thumb unmoved -> 01.
3. The Wizard can't believe it -> his face, the megaphone coming down -> 02 close-up.
4. Plan B: the band -> drum, tuba, cymbals and conductor all aimed at Phoenix from behind the bench, the bench shaking,
   Phoenix scrolling -> 03 wide.
5. Plan C: the Dragon lights the crate -> the puff of flame at the fuse, the rockets going up -> 04a very wide.
6. The sky says LOOK UP -> the letters over the skyline, the bench small below, pigeons off the roofs -> 04a.
7. The fake-out -> his thumb stops and his head lifts a little with LOOK UP glowing above him, then he swipes -> 04b.
8. The Wizard gives up and sits -> smoke-smudged, hat crooked, dropping onto the bench beside him -> 05 medium.
9. He texts "look up" -> his own screen with the bubble, the send whoosh, the music stops -> 05 (screen insert).
10. Ping -> Phoenix reads, looks up at once, smiles and waves "Oh, hi!" -> 06 close two-shot; the Wizard's eye twitches.
11. The Wizard points at the sky -> the sky behind them (only fading "LOOK U" sparks) in the same frame -> 07.
12. Phoenix shrugs and goes back to his phone; the Wizard face-plants; Reaper laughs, then everyone -> 07, last frame.

Payoff faces: Phoenix turns his head toward the Wizard (his left, screen right) by about 35° at 7.45 and up-and-left
by about 30° at 8.6: both stay inside 3/4. The Wizard faces Phoenix at 3/4 from 7.0 and points up without turning his
back. Reaper faces us from 9.3 to the end.

## Departures (and why)

- 04 is two shots, 04a very wide (3.60-4.75) and 04b a low medium on Phoenix with LOOK UP above him (4.75-5.60): the
  brief's fake-out (a 2° head lift) can't be seen on a 60 px tall Phoenix in the very wide, and LOOK UP stays in frame
  through both, so the letters read for well over 0.8 s.
- Panel 01: the Wizard stands on the bench seat to hold the megaphone at Phoenix's ear. Standing on the ground, his
  mouth would be about 30 cm below a seated Phoenix's ear.
- The megaphone lies under the bench from 1.9 s (not on it): the Dragon stands on the seat in 03 and the Wizard sits on it
  from 5.6 s.
- The band can't stand behind the bench (its backrest hides a standing potato's eyes): Reaper crashes the cymbals from
  the left end, the Dragon plays the tuba standing on the seat, Viking drums behind a smaller drum at the right end,
  the Wizard conducts from the front right. A push-in over 03 ends on the cymbals at Phoenix's ear.
- A cut-in on the fuse (04, 3.60-3.95 s) shows the Dragon's puff of flame and the launch; in the very wide the crate
  would be a few pixels.
- Phoenix's waving hand is back on his phone across the 8.4 s cut: on screen, every path from the wave back to the
  phone turned the glove over in one or two frames.
- "HEY!!!" is a comic text pop rather than a subtitle, so the promise line's subtitle can stay up long enough to read.
- The last frame holds Reaper, Phoenix and the face-down Wizard, not Viking and Dragon too: the five of them plus the
  bench need about 4.5 m of frame, and 9:16 at a readable size holds about 3 m (camfit at 10.45 s puts Viking's eyes
  at x 1,199 px when Reaper's are at 120 px). The Dragon is at the right edge as 07 opens.
