Place in every panel: a warm wooden pub at night. Dark timber beams with ivy and hanging plants, brass dome lamps,
bottle shelves behind the bar, a green banner with a potato emblem, dark green upper walls and wood panelling. The
toilet door at the left (dark wood, a white toilet pictogram, a brass handle, a little light box above it: green when
free, red when taken). A big arched window at the right onto a deep blue night street of lamplit houses. Small clear
tumblers (three empty, one with lemonade and a lemon slice). Samurai the bartender: navy shirt, white towel on his
shoulder, deadpan. Wizard in purple, Reaper in a dark hood; their coats are brown and dark grey.

## Plan and departures (reasons)

- **One continuous place, everyone standing.** The three stand at a high round table (top at belly height) in the
  middle of the room; the bar runs along the back with the Samurai behind it, the toilet door at the back left, the
  window wall a step further back at the right. Panel 1's table is kept (the brief's alternative); panels 5-8 draw the
  glasses on a bar, which here is the same table. Potatoes have very short legs, so a potato at a belly-high table
  reads as a person at a bar; stools would put the standing Samurai's face a head lower than Gunslinger's in the panel
  6-8 two-shot (the panels draw them level). "Sits down with a happy sigh" is a lean onto the table with one hand and a
  happy sigh.
- **Panel 1's Gunslinger is turned 3/4 toward us**, not seen from behind: frame 0 must show every face (hook rule).
  The three stand round the table in a half circle open to the camera; the Samurai shows between Reaper and the Wizard.
- **The toilet door opens inward** (hinged left, the leaf swings into the lit toilet room as in panel 3). He backs
  through it facing us with his cheeky look and pointing finger; on the cut to the nod (out of sight, behind the shut
  door) he is moved clear of the leaf's swing, so the door can close and open again without passing through him.
- **The door shot holds until 3.62 s** (the door shuts, the lock clicks, the light snaps red under the hit-stop), then
  the nod and the getaway are one wide shot from in front of the table (review A: the red light was a dot at the edge of
  the wide). The wide's toilet door sits at its left edge without the light box: the light is read in the door shot.
  The nod framed on its own showed only the backs of two heads and no door.
- **Panel 5 is a wide from the front right, lower** (as the panel): the empty table and a stool in front, Gunslinger
  peeking out of the toilet at the left, the Samurai behind the bar at the right holding the reader (review A). The
  Wizard and Reaper are out of the front door on that cut (the shot looks along their way out).
- **A wall clock** beside the toilet (a quarter past eleven) fills the bare wall of the wides (review A: the panels'
  room is lived-in).
- **The coats lie draped over the bar's front edge** (no coat stand or stool backs: the stools were cut with the
  table). Reaper's grey (lighter than the brief's dark grey, so it reads against his dark hood and the dark wood: review A), the Wizard's brown; they pick them up and throw them on as they tiptoe (panel 4 shows
  them already in coats).
- **Panels 6-8: Gunslinger at Reaper's old place, the Samurai beside him** at the table's back right (a body-width
  apart), both behind the table, the camera over the table's front and a little above them so the window and the street show
  above their heads. The reader comes from the bar: the Samurai picks it up while Gunslinger is in the toilet and
  holds it behind the bar through the peek (panel 5 has him there); on the cut to the two-shot he has come round the
  bar's end and is a step from the table, sets it on the table beside Gunslinger and pushes it a short way with one finger to stop in front of
  Gunslinger (the brief: "a short way with one finger from beside him, no long curling slide"). The slide is the
  reader's own tween with an ease-out (constant friction deceleration, flat on the top), not a physics body: let go as
  a body it starts inside the hand's collision ball and is flung off the table.
- **The reader is a little bigger than a real one** (15 x 22 cm) so 84.00 reads at phone size; it is smaller in frame
  than panels 7-8 draw it, because the vertical frame has to hold the two faces and the street behind them too.
- **Gunslinger's walk back is cut**: he steps out of the toilet doorway in the peek shot, and on the cut to the
  two-shot he is a step from the table (2.4 m in the 0.6 s the story gives it was a dash, not a pleased stroll).
- **Panel 6's friends stand at the window instead of walking away outside**: the street is a step up, and the planted
  gait reads a raised body as off the floor, so a walk there had no footsteps and its lean jumped. They snicker to
  each other, then wave (panels 7-8).
- **He sets the lemonade down hard at the bill** (7.5 s) instead of holding it: his right hand is free for the
  two-finger hat tip at the wink, and the last frame has no prop floating at his side.
- **The window is a 3D window wall** at the back right (the painted back wall has none), with a painted night street
  (assets/plates/pub-street-night.png) behind it, so the Wizard and Reaper can stand outside it and wave (panels 7-8).
  Their legs are hidden by the wall under the sill. The street is a step up from the pub floor (45 cm), so their
  faces show in the window above the two heads at the table in the vertical two-shot.
- **The toilet door is a wide one (1.25 m)**: Gunslinger's hat brim is wider than a real door leaves room for, and the
  leaf has to swing past it when he pulls it open from inside (panel 5).
- **Music**: the brief asks for an Irish fiddle reel; beat.mjs has no fiddle, so the bed is the nearest style it has
  (seeded per clip), quiet while they tiptoe, cut dead on the reader's stop.
- **Subtitles** sit inside y 470-1360, not at the bottom as drawn.
- **The Samurai taps with his right hand, turned to us** (panel 8 draws the finger on the reader): from the two-shot's
  side his left arm, on our side, crossed his own chin (review A). The push is a flick: the reader slides on alone
  and his left hand drops to his side (held out after it, his arm crossed his chin through the shock).
- **Motion ticks at their feet** (panel 4) are little dust ticks and a bounce on every other step of the tiptoe out.
- **The Samurai's towel moves to his camera-side shoulder on the cut to the two-shot** (6.3 s story): turned to
  Gunslinger, on the other shoulder it hid behind his head for the whole payoff (review B); the panels draw it on the
  side we see. The cut hides the switch.
- **During the shock he glances up to the window** (7.95-8.35 s story) and back to the Samurai before the wink, so
  the viewer finds the waving friends because he does (review B). The reader's 84.00 stays lit through the shock and
  flashes once with a little squash on the Samurai's tap.
