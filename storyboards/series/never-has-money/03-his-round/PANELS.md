## Departures (reasons)

- **The wide looks across the bar from the bartender's side** (as EP01 did across the diner counter). Panel 1 draws a
  table with the friends facing us and panel 5 draws the stools from the customers' side; panels 6-8 draw the bar top
  between us and Gunslinger. Frame 0 must show every face, and the brief says the friends walk "behind the table, never
  in front of Gunslinger's stool": so the three sit on stools on the far side of the bar facing us, the tumblers on the
  bar in front of them (panel 1's "table" is the bar, as Jonny's note suggests), and the room (toilet door left, bottle
  shelves + banner centre, arched window right) is the far wall behind them. The Samurai stands behind the bar on our
  side at the right end, turned 3/4 toward them, so his face reads.
- **Panel 5's stools-in-the-foreground view** becomes the same wide with the stools empty behind the bar.
- **The toilet door is on a short partition at the left**, 0.8 m in front of the far wall, so his dash is ~1.1 m.
- **The friends leave by a front door just off frame right** (the bell rings there) and reappear outside in the window.
- **Subtitles** between y 470 and 1360, not at the bottom as drawn (brief).
- **The card reader's screen tilts up** so both Gunslinger and the camera read "84.00" (panel 7 draws it facing us).
