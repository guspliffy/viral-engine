blue sky and green trees, a stone fireplace with a lit fire behind the cast (panel 1), shelves with jars and potted

## The story chain (what happens -> what the viewer must see -> the shot)

1. It's Viking's birthday and the friends are giving presents -> party hats, cake, garland, the horn blast, the big
   purple present going into Viking's arms, the black box set on top -> P1 wide, all four faces, both presents.
2. Gunslinger has nothing -> every head turns to him at "And yours?", his empty mittens shown palms up -> P1 wide.
3. He has no money -> the pocket pat, the empty wallet, the moth -> P2 medium (series block), Viking with the presents
   at the edge so we remember who's waiting.
4. He gets an idea -> a finger up; he pulls a folded paper out of his bandana and the pen out of its knot; the crate is
   beside him from frame 0 (visible in P1), so sitting on it needs no new place -> P3 medium.
5. He makes the gift himself -> paper flat on his knee, the pen scribbling, tongue out; a tear-off -> P3 medium.
6. The gift is a FREE HUG coupon -> held up facing us, the words readable at 360 px -> P4 close + hit-stop 1.
7. The friends think it's a joke -> Wizard snickers into his mitten, Reaper rolls his eyes, they glance at each other ->
   P5 (both faces in frame round Viking).
8. Viking is moved -> he takes the coupon (we see the hand-over), reads it, lip trembles, tears -> P5 close.
9. The cheapest gift wins -> he drops the expensive presents (we see them fall onto the rug) and bear-hugs Gunslinger,
   feet off the floor -> P6 full figures + hit-stop 2.
10. Wizard's expensive gift is ignored -> his jaw drops at the purple box on the rug; Reaper calmly rescues his own box
    and dusts it -> P7 wide, W and R in front of the hug.
11. Gunslinger wins again -> he winks at the Wizard over Viking's shoulder, "Next year's on me!"; the Wizard twitches ->
    P8 close, the Wizard in frame because Gunslinger looks at him.

## Departures (reasons)

- **Everyone stands at least 0.85 m from everyone else** (a potato is about 0.8 m wide; at the panels' spacing the
  8 Nov layout had bodies 55-67 % into each other). So the room is wider than the panel: the 9:16 wides (P1, P5a, P7)
  cut the Wizard and Reaper at their edges, as panel 1 does, and no close shot holds more than two of them.
- **Gunslinger stands at Viking's right (screen left) all clip**, so panels 1-5 are as drawn (left to right: Wizard,
  Gunslinger, Viking, Reaper) and the hug in panels 6-7 is mirrored (Viking at the right, Gunslinger at the left). The
  panels swap the two between panel 5 and panel 6; in one continuous room that needs a walk round each other that the
  story doesn't have. Panel 8 matches again: Viking turns slowly with him in the hug, so by the wink Viking's hair and
  shoulder are at the right and Gunslinger winks over it.
- **Panel 1: Viking stands three-quarters to us, turned toward the Wizard, at frame 0** (the box goes from the
  Wizard's palms into his arms between them, and his face reads in the first frame), then turns to Reaper for the
  black box and to us at 1.1 (a 0.4 s turn: at 0.25 s the box's far end swung 20 cm in one drawing). After the push the Wizard steps back to Gunslinger's left
  (0.55-1.0 s), out of the shots on Gunslinger; at 4.15 (while the camera is on the coupon) he steps in at the front
  left, where he stays to the end.
- **Panel 2 from the front left, panel 3 slightly from the right and a little above** (panel 3 is slightly from the
  left): from the left the Wizard, who stands at Gunslinger's left, would fill the frame. Viking stays at the right
  edge of both.
- **The pen comes out of his bandana's knot**, not his hat band (the brief): his short arm can't get past the wide brim
  without going through it (EP02's contact fail). The paper comes out of the bandana too.
- **He writes with the pen in his left mitten**, the paper in his right (panel 3 has the pen in his right): the right
  hand pulls the paper out first and keeps it.
- **After the tear he hops forward off the crate** (0.7 m toward us) to hold the coupon up: from where the crate stands,
  Viking's presents were in front of the coupon.
- **Panel 5 is two shots**: P5a, the wide (4.6-5.2: the Wizard snickers into his mitten at the front left, Reaper rolls
  his eyes at the right, they glance at each other across Viking, Gunslinger holds out the coupon), then P5b, close on
  Viking (5.2-6.0: he takes it, reads, tears, "Best... gift... EVER!"). At 0.85 m spacing the Wizard and Reaper are
  1.4-1.6 m from Viking, too far for one close.
- **Viking takes the coupon in his right mitten and keeps the presents on his left arm** (mirror of panel 5), because
  Gunslinger is on his right.
- **Panel 1: Viking turns and holds the purple present out to Reaper**, who comes in along it and sets the black box
  on top, then folds his mittens in front of his belly for the rest of the clip: from the side Reaper's mittens would be
  in Viking's fur (the 8 Nov layout), and his arms at his sides reach into Viking at 0.9 m.
- **Panel 6 is a side hug, side by side and both facing out**: Viking scoops Gunslinger up on his right forearm, the
  way you carry a child on your hip, his left fist up in joy; Gunslinger rides 0.4 m off the rug (his feet dangle in
  the low medium-wide) with his arm over Viking's near shoulder. A front bear hug puts two pairs of short arms through
  two round bodies, and a mitten round his back sat 16-29 cm inside him in the 8 Nov layouts (a short arm can't reach
  round a round body); side by side with a forearm under him keeps them close without intersecting.
- **The coupon is held by its lower corners** (panel 4 holds it at its sides): at phone-hold height the mitten covers
  FREE. On his knee he writes on it at 0.85 of its size (the folded half), and it opens to full size as he holds it up.
- **The coupon flutters out of Viking's mitten onto the rug as he grabs Gunslinger** (panel 6 keeps it in his mitten
  behind Gunslinger's back): held there the sheet sits inside Gunslinger's body (86 % in the 8 Nov layout). The hug is
  what the coupon was for; in panel 7 it lies on the rug beside the expensive presents.
- **Panel 7: Reaper takes his black box up in both mittens and holds it safe at his chest, deadpan**, instead of
  dusting it: dusting needs one mitten to let go of a two-hand box hold mid-shot. The box flies off the purple present
  as Viking lets go and lands on the rug behind Viking (the physics bake decides where); Reaper steps to it, scoops it
  up with his back to us and turns to us holding it (7.5-8.85). Picked up in front of Viking, his mittens went into
  Viking's costume and the box's fall was deflected by his robe (layout rounds 19-21).
- **Panel 8: the Wizard hops in toward the hug at 8.2 s and faces us at the bottom left, head tipped back to stare up
  at Gunslinger** (his eyes up and to the right): turned to Gunslinger his own wide brim hides his face from this
  camera, and the twitch is the payoff; his eyes sit above the app's caption band (review A).
- **Panel 8 is a little wider and at eye level** (`w` 1.8, 14° down; the panel's close is from above): the three
  faces fill the close high shot, so every place for "Next year's on me!" sat on a face or a body (caption-overlap
  60-100 %, check round 1). Opened up, the line sits over the fireplace above his hat (4 % covered), with the
  Wizard's twitch at the bottom left, Gunslinger's wink in the middle and Viking at the right, as drawn.
- **"Best... gift... EVER!" sits above Viking's hair**, not in the middle of panel 5: lower it covered his face
  (70-95 %, check round 1); the close-up is framed a little higher for it, and the subtitle ends at the cut to the hug.
- **The room is a painted plate behind the 3D floor, rug, crate, table and cake** (the brief's allowed fallback): no
  3D living-room set exists in the library yet.
- **No sweat drop, no "!" or shock marks** (panels 4, 6, 8): Jonny rejected reaction signs over characters on 8 Nov.
  The Wizard's twitching eyelid carries panel 8.
- **No sniffle or sob sound**: neither is on the sound whitelist; Viking's tears are silent under his line.
- **Anime timing with no push** (`animeTiming: { push: 1, slow: 5 }`): the pocket pat and the dodge chain into one
  2.7 s key segment, and the default push ran Gunslinger's drawings up to 0.6 s ahead of the clock (a 23 cm body
  jump at 3.70 s in the layout). Held drawings on 2s keep the look without the drift.
