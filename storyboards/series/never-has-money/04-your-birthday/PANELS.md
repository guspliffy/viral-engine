## The story chain (what happens -> what the viewer must see -> the shot that shows it)

1. The Wizard pushes the huge purple present into Viking's arms -> the box, the Wizard's mittens on it, Viking's beam, at frame 0 with sound -> P01 wide.
2. Reaper sets the neat black box on top -> his glove setting it down on the purple box -> P01.
3. Gunslinger has nothing -> his empty mittens (no present anywhere near him) while the others hold theirs -> P01, 0.6-1.4.
4. "And yours?" -> the Wizard's eyes and turn to Gunslinger, then Viking's and Reaper's: every eyeline lands on him -> P01, 1.1-1.5.
5. The pocket pat finds a moth -> the open EMPTY wallet, the moth, Viking expectant at the edge -> P02 (series block).
6. "Wait!" and the dodge -> his finger up, then he hurries to the crate, sits, pulls paper and pen, scribbles turned half away, Viking watching -> P03 (the crate stands at the front left from frame 0, so it has a place).
7. The coupon -> FREE HUG and the doodle readable (a quarter of the frame width) at chest height, his grin, hit-stop 4.2 -> P04.
8. The friends react -> the Wizard snickers into his mitten, Reaper rolls his eyes, they glance at each other -> P05 (behind Viking).
9. Viking takes it and is moved -> the coupon passing from Gunslinger's mitten to Viking's (Gunslinger's hat at the left edge of P05, his arm reaching in), Viking reading it, the lip trembling, the tears, "Best... gift... EVER!" (music out 5.2-5.9) -> P05.
10. The expensive presents fall -> both boxes leaving his arm and thudding on the rug (the black box bounces off the purple) -> P06, 6.0-6.4.
11. The bear hug lifts him -> Gunslinger's boots off the rug, Viking's arms round his back, hit-stop 6.1 -> P06.
12. The bill lands: the big present is ignored -> the Wizard staring at his purple box on the rug, jaw dropping; Reaper picks his up and dusts it -> P07.
13. The wink -> over Viking's shoulder, the two-finger hat tip, the wink, the moth lifting; the Wizard's twitch in the foreground (he spent the most) -> P08, hard cut at 10.00.

## Departures (reasons)

- **Rows swap during the clip.** The panels have the Wizard and Reaper in front in 01 and 07 but behind Viking in 05.
  So the cast moves with a cause each time: frame 0 the Wizard and Reaper have just stepped in to hand over their
  presents, and by 1.0 they step back beside the other two (giving room to the birthday boy), which also lets them look
  across at Gunslinger at 1.1 with their faces to us (from in front of him they would have turned their backs to the
  camera). At 2.8 Gunslinger hurries forward to the crate at the front left to write in private; the Wizard shuffles in
  behind to peek (3.35) and Reaper takes Viking's place when Viking steps forward to take the coupon (4.6-5.0). So in
  05 Viking is in front with the Wizard behind his left shoulder and Reaper behind his right, as drawn. At 6.3 the
  Wizard and Reaper come round the hug to their fallen presents, so they are in front again for 07 and 08.
- **The coupon is handed over Gunslinger's shoulder in 05**: he stands at the front left, a metre from Viking, his hat
  at the left edge of the frame (an over-the-shoulder close); closer, his hat brim covered Viking's face (stills).
- **The hug swings.** Viking reaches Gunslinger from behind his right, lifts him and swings him round behind himself
  (6.0-6.6), so 06 and 07 have Viking at the left and Gunslinger at the right as drawn; the rocking then turns them on
  (8.0-8.7) until Viking's back is to the front-left camera and Gunslinger, lifted higher, looks out over Viking's
  left shoulder for the wink (08). Side by side, angled in (cheek to cheek), both faces read in 06-07; face to face
  they were both in profile (stills).
- **The cake is not in 01.** Drawn at the bottom left of 01, in front of everyone. There it would stand in the
  coupon close (04), Viking's close (05) and the presents' landing spot (07); the bunting, hats, rosette and presents
  already say birthday at frame 0. It stands on its table at the front left, off frame.
- **The crate stands at the front left, just out of 01's frame**: it is in 03 as he hurries to it, and
  in 03 the camera is on his left side (his left, as drawn), so Viking, who steps closer to peek, is behind him at
  the right.
- **The presents land further forward (1.4-1.8 m) than a plain drop.** Viking flings them off his arm as he lunges, so
  in 07 they lie in front of the Wizard and Reaper, who face us as they look down at them.
- **The Wizard and Reaper come round the hug (6.25-7.2)**: the Wizard down the left between the crate and the hug,
  Reaper round the right. In 08 the Wizard stands in the front left, staring ahead stunned while Gunslinger, lifted
  higher, winks down at him.
