## Departures (reasons)

- **Panel 1 is shot from the counter side, not the customer side.** In the drawing the counter runs diagonally and the
  friends turn 3/4; potatoes' faces only read from the front, and frame 0 must show every face (hook rule). So the
  wide looks across the counter at the three friends (the side panels 2 and 5-8 use), with the Samurai at the
  counter's right end, turned 3/4 toward them, sliding the tray. Panel 4's reverse angle (over Gunslinger's shoulder
  at the Samurai, the kitchen wall behind) is kept as its own shot.
- **Panel 8:** Reaper is seated right of Gunslinger, so his annoyed face is at the right edge, not bottom left.
- **Subtitles** sit at y ~1180 (inside TikTok's safe zone), not at the very bottom as drawn.
- **The button is teal and 6.4 cm across** (drawn brown in the panels): brown vanished against Gunslinger's hat and
  glove at phone size, and held props must contrast with the costume (CLAUDE.md, writing clips 6).
- **Panel 4 is a high reverse** over the gap between Gunslinger and Reaper: lower, Reaper's hood or Gunslinger's hat
  hid the tray and the button.
