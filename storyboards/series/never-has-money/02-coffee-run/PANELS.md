# Never Has Money 02 "coffee run": Jonny's eight panels

Board: /mnt/project-files/video-scripts/2026-11-05-never-has-money/storyboards/02-coffee-run/storyboard-eight-panels.png
(2x4 grid). Brief: video-scripts/2026-11-05-never-has-money/02-coffee-run-ENGINE-PROMPT.md in the project files.

Place in every panel: a small, cosy shared-flat kitchen in warm morning sun. Open wooden shelves with glass jars and
mugs, hanging pothos plants trailing from the top of the frame, a window at the right with blue sky and a town outside,
cream walls and tiles. A tall rounded retro fridge in egg-yolk yellow at the left, its door covered top to bottom in
pale yellow IOU sticky notes ("IOU" and a little cup doodle in blue marker), layered like feathers, coloured magnet
pins on a few. A wooden table in the foreground with a red-checked cloth, a wooden bowl of red apples and a potted
plant. Paper takeaway cups with a kraft sleeve, a potato logo and a white lid; a grey pulp cup carrier.

## Departures (reasons)

- **The Wizard stands at the right front**, not the front left as panel 1 draws him. The brief has him "entering from
  the right foreground", and at the left front he stood between the camera and the fridge in panels 5-7 (draft stills,
  7 Nov). Reaper waits at the back right by the counter, so the fridge at the left is always clear.
- **Panel 1 is a medium-wide, not a wide** (reach rules v2, B1, 7 Nov: frame 0 shows the troublemaker and the victim
  medium-wide with the trouble already happening): the camera is lower and closer, so the fridge shows at the left
  edge (its right half, the notes readable as yellow) and the three fill the lower half. The Wizard's line starts at
  0.0 s (B2).
- **Panel 7 is shot from higher and wider** than the panel's front view: from the front, Gunslinger and the heap in
  the foreground covered the open fridge and Reaper. From above, the open door, the heap and Reaper reaching in read
  together.
- **The tray is already on the table at frame 0**, his right palm out over it: he has just set it down (the front door
  clunks shut off screen right). It still has his cup and Gunslinger's in it.
- **The handle is on the door's right edge**, not its left as panels 4-6 draw it. The brief hinges the door on the
  left so it swings toward camera-left and clears the frame (panel 7 shows it open at the left edge), and a door's
  handle is on its opening edge. Gunslinger stands on that side, so the notes fall on him.
- **Frame 0: Reaper already holds his cup**, as panel 1 draws him; the brief's "Reaper takes his cup too" happened
  just before the clip.
- **Gunslinger takes his cup with his left hand**, the one nearer the tray, and writes on the note held in front of
  his chest, not on his knee (a potato has no knee to write on). The note comes out of his bandana alone, not as a
  pad. The note is in his right hand and the marker in his left (the right hand goes to the handle).
- **The marker comes out of his bandana's knot**, not his hat band (the brief): his short arm can't get past that wide
  brim to the band without going through it (the contact check fails it), so it is tucked next to the note.
- **The Wizard points across in front of himself at the fridge** in panel 4 instead of jabbing a thumb over his
  shoulder: from the front camera a raised thumb sat right over Reaper's face behind him.
- **Panel 4 is a front two-shot** of the Wizard and Gunslinger with the fridge behind at the left and Reaper between
  them at the back, not an over-the-shoulder: from behind Gunslinger the camera sat in his hat, and Reaper's nod was
  out of the frame.
- **Panel 6 is two shots**: Gunslinger at the handle (patting it), then a cut to the Wizard's facepalm. With the
  Wizard at the right front, one frame can't hold both him and the handle at a size that reads on a phone.
- **After the pats Gunslinger steps back in front of the door and turns to us, chest out, proud**, and Reaper takes
  his place at the handle. There is no room for Reaper to pass between Gunslinger and the Wizard (the contact check
  had their bodies 46 % into each other), and with his back to the door the avalanche hits him from behind, which is
  the bigger laugh. So in panel 7 Gunslinger is in front of the fridge at the left (the panel has him at the right)
  and Reaper at its right side reaches in for the milk.
- **Panel 8 is a three-shot from above their heads**: Gunslinger in the heap in front of the open fridge (left),
  Reaper pouring behind him (middle), the Wizard sipping (right). The panel has the Wizard left and Reaper right, both
  in the foreground; here the Wizard is on the right all clip, and the higher camera keeps the wink clear.
- **His first try is the middle of the door, not the top** (the brief): to reach the top his short arm has to go up
  past his own hat brim, and every path either went through the brim or tipped the brim into the door (contact check,
  7 Nov). He tries the middle as high as his arm goes, then the side, then bends down to the bottom.
- **The fridge door stays open at the end**: Reaper has the milk in one hand and his cup in the other.
- **Buried to the hat brim** (the brief) rather than to the chest (panel 7): the heap slumps to his chest at the wink
  (panel 8), so his face is clear by 9.0.
