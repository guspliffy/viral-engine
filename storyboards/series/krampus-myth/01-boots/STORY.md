## Facts (standard Alpine folklore)
- Krampusnacht is the night of 5 December, the eve of Saint Nicholas' Day; Austria, Bavaria, South Tyrol and nearby.
- Children clean their boots and set them out; Saint Nicholas fills good kids' boots (oranges, nuts, chocolate);
  the Krampus brings the birch rod (Rute), in stories carries the worst kids off in a basket on his back.
- Saint Nicholas has the golden book (Goldenes Buch) with every child's year in it.
- The Krampus: fur, goat horns, long red tongue, cowbells and chains so you hear him coming. The name is traced
  to Krampen, "claw".
- Krampus runs (Krampuslauf, Perchtenlauf) still go through Alpine towns every late November / December, often hundreds
  of masked runners. "Every Tuesday" fries and the swap are our jokes, not claims.
