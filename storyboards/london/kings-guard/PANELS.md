## Departures (with reasons)

- **The corner is at the back right of the forecourt, and the camera keeps looking toward the palace.** The painted
  place exists only in the painting's direction, so the board's reverse angle for panels 7-8 can't be shot. The board's
  screen layout is kept exactly (wall behind Reaper, pier on the right, Phoenix leaning in from the right); Phoenix sits
  with his back to the box in panels 5-6, so the guard leaves unseen.
- **Panel 6 keeps the wide camera** instead of a new angle (a cut to a framing this close reads as a jump cut).
- **The Wizard stays back right in panel 6** rather than walking to the camera's foreground (his hat isn't in the
  board's bottom-right corner).
- **The scythe stays in Reaper's hand while he laughs** (tilted onto the wall) instead of being let go: a costume
  scythe that is let go and picked up again in the 0.1 s snap would pop.
- **Crossed eyes**: the face rig has one gaze for both eyes, so the silly face uses tiny pupils, a wide open mouth and
  the tongue prop.
- **Panel 7's "doubled over"** is a shallow bend (pitch 10) with a deep crouch, side sway and a crooked bearskin: a
  deeper bend tips the bearskin over his face (review A and B).
- **Panel 5-6**: Phoenix stays flat in the puddle until the guard is past him, then sits up. Sitting up straight away,
  he hid the whole march at phone size.
