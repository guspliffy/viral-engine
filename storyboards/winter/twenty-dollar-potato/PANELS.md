## Where the clip departs from the panels, and why

- **The lid is hinged on the box's side, not its far edge.** In panels 4 and 5 the lid stands up behind the potato. A
  lid hinged at the back would stand between the camera and the potato in the front wides (panel 6), so the box
  opens like a book: the lid stands to the right of the potato in the wides and to the left in Viking's view.
- **The potato turns to the friends in panel 6.** In his own view he faces Viking; in the wide he turns toward the
  line (the cause of his face being toward us).
- **The painted set is plaza, not the rink up close.** The painting is one picture with the rink, tree and towers
  behind a snowy stone plaza; a strip of plaza shows under the counter in the wides. The market's other stalls in the
  panels' background aren't there (a painting that put huts behind the line read as a village; kept for a later set).
- **The second box appears on the cut** to the last shot, already under the Samurai's glove.
- **The box is hugged on his chest from the last cut** (the same box, worn, so one arm holds it and the other is free).
- **The potato is a fifth of the cast's size**, so it fits in a takeaway box the cast can hold.
- **The wide is high, as the panels are**: the camera stands 2.3 m up and 5.3 m back looking down a little, so the
  friends further back rise over the heads in front (Phoenix filming over Viking's right shoulder). The counter is at
  the cast's belts (0.3 m), as in the panels, so Viking's belt and fur show, and it ends before the Wizard so his
  cocoa trickles onto the plaza in view.
- **Reaper peeks over Viking's left shoulder from the very back**, not at the right edge (panels 1, 6, 8). In a 9:16
  frame the Wizard's hat covered him on the right in every placement that stayed in frame; at the back left his face
  reads in every wide. He is still last in the line.
- **In Viking's own view his gloves stay off the box** (panels 4 and 5 show them on its near corners). From the eye
  camera the short potato arms reach the box from the sides and read as dark shapes over the frame's corners, so he
  lets go after the lid pops and just stares into the box. He takes it in both gloves again only to cradle it (panel 7).
- **The potato's topping is a little bigger than his fifth size** (0.3) so the sour cream hat and the cheese read in
  Viking's view.
