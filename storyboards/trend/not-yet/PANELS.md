## Where the clip departs from the panels, and why

- Panels 4 and 5 are mirrored. In panel 1 the door is at the left and Reaper comes in from there. So the Wizard dives
  right, fires back left, and the bolts hit the lamp and shelf beside the door. In panel 4 the Wizard fires to the right.
- In panel 5 the Wizard stands in the corner by the calendar instead of on the sofa, so his staff leans against the
  wall there.
- In panel 8 the TV is out of frame, just right of the camera, and they look at it. A vertical frame can't show the TV
  beside them without its back hiding their laps.
