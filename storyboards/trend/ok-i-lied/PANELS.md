## Where the clip departs from the panels, and why

- The board's panels are landscape; the clip is 9:16. The trio fills the width, so the extra height is scenery (sky,
  palms, the burning hotel) and the caption sits above the heads. The trio is smaller in the frame than in the board.
- Reaper stands a little behind the other two, and Phoenix's wings are spread to about half: at full spread Phoenix's
  flame wing covers Reaper's face (the panels' wings sit behind him, which the 3D wing can't do side by side).
- The run ends with the three a bit apart at the lens rather than packed shoulder to shoulder: closer, Reaper's
  scythe goes into Phoenix.
- The rain has no sound (no rain sound in the whitelist); the music turns sad from the car park on instead.
- The flash at the edges in panel 8 is speed lines only: a flash over the whole frame washed the faces out.
- Shortened to 6 s (Jonny, 4 Nov: "by second 1.5 should be the we weren't at the beach scene"; before that a 1.25x
  ffmpeg speed-up of the 10 s cut, 1 Nov). Photos 1-4 now last 0.7-0.8 s each (the car park at 1.4 s); the Dragon,
  the sneeze and the run keep their real-time pace (0.6 + 0.7 + 0.7 + 1.0 s). The table's times are the old 10 s cut.
