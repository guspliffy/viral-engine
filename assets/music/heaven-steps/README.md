# heaven-steps (Potato Desk, 100 points for heaven) score

`heaven-steps-score.ogg`: made with ffmpeg by `storyboards/series/potato-desk/heaven-steps/make-score.mjs` from
library takes (same source and licence as assets/music/ai, see its manifest), each loudness-matched, laid on the
clip's seconds and slowed by the final 1.25x speed-up so it plays at its own tempo: `elegant-reveal/elegant-reveal-02`
(heaven's gate, to "step 2"), `spooky/spooky-02` (hell's door, to "humility"), `drama-swell/drama-swell-01` (the gold
escalator, to "humble"), `elegant-reveal/elegant-reveal-02` again (the loop back to the gate). Replaces the rough
draft's sneaky bed after Jonny's story check (20 Nov 2026).
