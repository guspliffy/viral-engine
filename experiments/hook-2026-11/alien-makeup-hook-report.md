## Cold read, round 1 (28 Oct, openings framed as the brief: extreme close-up on the brush; C on the frontal alien close-up)

All three scored 3/5 ("cute characters, premise still unclear/predictable") and all three asked for the same change:
pull the first frame back so both characters, the brush and the green face are in it.

- **A** (no text): understood "a makeup session" only at 2.0-2.8 s (the wide shot with the mirror). "0.00 and 0.40 are too tightly cropped to read anything but a brush and a blob of green." Score: late.
- **B** (caption): understood "friend doing Halloween makeup, it's bad" at 1.3 s. Expected: the friend sees it in the mirror and is horrified. Score: understood (just).
- **C** (result first): readable as makeup at 0.8 s; noticed the paint "tilting off, exposing the orange smiling face" at 2.8 s. "The opener is an abstract green blob with zero context." Score: late.

Useful side note: every reader took the green paint for the friend's face. That is the setup the twist needs (the reveal is that it was on the glass).

Change made: the opening shot (A/B shot 01, C's first shot) is now a close two-shot like the storyboard's first panel.

- **A** (no text): 2/5, "cute art, but premise invisible". Read the Astronaut as a green alien; saw a tool and a "magnifying glass", no setup. Score: missed.
- **B** (caption "When your friend does your Halloween makeup"): 3/5, "premise clear, payoff fairly predictable". Expected the friend to see it in the mirror and be horrified (the real payoff, the paint coming off with the visor, stays a surprise). Score: understood.
- **C** (result first): 2/5, "cute, but no hook". Read as "an alien and a witch, one inspecting something with a magnifying glass". Score: missed.
