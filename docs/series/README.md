# Series: what each one is and what it must keep

The files next to this one are each series' format as it was written (copied from the project's video-scripts,
15 Nov 2026). They are older than the notes below: where they differ, the notes below and `docs/rules/story.md` win.
Episode generators live in `storyboards/series/<series>/`; the last episode is the pattern the next one copies
(reuse its set, props and music). Status is as of 15 Nov 2026.

## Main formats (best data so far)

- **There's a catch** (`what-if.md`, `storyboards/series/what-if/`): a superpower and its price. Narrator (voice 1)
  opens "What if you could <power>?", the turn is "But there's a catch." The catch is the story's own consequence,
  shown big, never a fun fact. 30-35 s, real-speed action from frame 0, music that drops or flips at the turn,
  commentator on by default, "Sounds perfect, right?" over the 10x moment. Spud and his friends or fighters.
  Episodes 01-07, 09b done; next powers: teleport, strength. Best example: 07 fire rewind.
- **Ancient aliens** (`storyboards/series/ancient-aliens/`, kit in `kit/`): 2-4 min mystery films, Spud hosts with
  Reaper's voice, real facts. Landscape 16:9 film over 3:00 for YouTube, vertical parts for TikTok, each part with its
  own hook. Three pillars: detailed beautiful pictures, interesting commentary with each fact shown happening, music
  that supports each scene from the first animatic. But/Therefore outline, one science staircase per episode.
  01 pyramids, 02 Stonehenge done; 03 Atlantis in work.
- **Friends try to** (`friends-try-to.md`): the friend group tries a real task (move a Stonehenge stone, put up the
  Christmas tree). 01-08 done.

## Other running formats

- **You wake up mid ...** (`storyboards/series/you-wake-up-mid/`): Spud wakes in the middle of a disaster (plane
  crash, sinking car, zombie apocalypse), stake + clock first ("Ninety seconds to live"), realistic. EP03 loops: wakes
  in bed, sleeps, loops to frame 0.
- **Survive it** (`survive-it.md`): realistic, no absurdity, 5-6 real survival steps acted and named, one chain per
  episode, a zombie episode has a real fight. Six-line outline before any build.
- **Fight, Flight or Freeze** (`fight-flight-freeze.md`): one extreme situation, three friends, each picks
  fight, flight or freeze; all three outcomes in one video, every outcome with a visible cause.
- **Share or Steal** (`share-or-steal.md`): two fighters, a prize, share or steal; rules shown (not
  spoken) by 3.0 s, a 3-5 s timer at most, the viewer picks a side.
- **Hot Potato** (`storyboards/series/hot-potato/`): deadly cute game show, opener A.
- **Doomscroll to survive** (`storyboards/series/doomscroll/`): pilot "Sprout Man".
- **Happiness** (new, 15 Nov): Spud looks for happiness, narrator leads, one life question per episode never answered
  directly, ends on a sunset or nature smile. Pilot "Does money make you happy?" in work.
