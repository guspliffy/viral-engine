## What this series is for

The account's new direction is adult (18-34), not kid-friendly: real stakes, a dark edge, and only a little humour from weird twists. Share or Steal is a trust game with a deep fryer: two friends, one pile of gold, one secret choice each. Viewers argue in the comments about what they would pick.

## The rules (shown in every episode, never read out: format v2)

| Both pick | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| SHARE + SHARE | They split the gold and both walk away |
| STEAL + SHARE | The stealer takes all the gold; the one who shared goes in the fryer (Jonny's rule) |
| STEAL + STEAL | Both go in the fryer (confirmed by Jonny, 10 Nov) |

Why double steal is deadly: your own card only decides the gold, the other one's card decides whether you live. You can't save yourself, you can only trust.

## What stays the same in every episode

1. **About 14-16 s** (v2; v1 was 20-24 s), 9:16, 1080 x 1920, whole frame sharp. Potato cast only.
2. **One set, reused for all five episodes:** a dark game-show studio. One spotlit table, the gold pile in the middle of it, two chairs facing each other, and, from v2 on, a big open vat of oil UNDER a steel grate that the table and both chairs stand on, each chair on its own hatch (v1, EP01: a deep fryer behind the table), so it's in every wide shot (orange under-light from the oil, steam, a constant low hiss). Each player has two cards face down: SHARE and STEAL.
3. **Never the original show's look:** no golden balls, no show name, no host on camera. Our props are cards and a fryer. On-screen name is always "Share or Steal".
4. **Host = narrator voice 5** ("trailer narrator", recipe narrator-trailer in engine/voice/voices.json; on branch what-if-01-pause-time until PR #180 merges). He is a voice from the dark, never seen. The players speak gibberish with captions as always.
5. **Rules shown, not spoken, done by 3.0 s** (v2): three small picture cards and the blinking hatch lights, see "Format v2". v1 had the host read them in one breath (5.5 s in EP01: too long).
6. **Countdown: 3 to 5 s at most** (v2: a big 4 to 0 in the HUD style; v1's 10 to 0 lost half the viewers, Jonny). One plea, the 10x one, and the other player's answer. No stair of three pleas.
7. **Cards slide forward at 0, then flip one at a time.** The first reveal is the fake-out, the second is the truth.
8. **The fryer stays off screen in the bad moment:** the loser drops straight down through his own hatch in the grate (v2; v1: the chair tipped back through a trapdoor behind the table), then splash, sizzle and a column of steam up through the open hatch. The camera never looks down into the oil while someone is in it. Nothing graphic. No silent freeze before any reveal (pause test dropped); the countdown, the hiss and the music carry the tension.
9. **End card** with one dry line about what happened after, then the closing question "Share or steal?" and a hard cut.
10. All house rules: frame 0 shows the title and both players with the fryer already bubbling (trouble already happening), sound at 0.0, stakes visible by 1.5 s, characters react to each other, eyebrows on every reaction, no reaction signs, Story Ladder (promise breaks in the first second, fake-out before the payoff), MrBeast playbook (frame 0 = title, stair-steps, one wow).

## EP01: Gunslinger vs Viking (built in format v1, merged 10 Nov as PR #191; NO v2 re-cut: Jonny, 11 Nov)

Gunslinger is the friend who never has money and owes Viking. Viking is big, loud and soft-hearted.

- **Pleas:** Gunslinger: "I'll share, I swear!" Then he turns his empty pockets out. Then the 10x plea: he drops to his knees on the table and sobs, hugging Viking's arm. Viking's face goes from suspicious to soft.
- **Reveal 1:** Viking flips SHARE. Warm music swells; it looks like friendship wins.
- **Reveal 2:** Gunslinger flips STEAL. The music cuts. Viking's chair tips back: splash, sizzle, steam. Gunslinger pulls the whole pile into his arms.
- **End card:** "Gunslinger spent it all by Friday."
- **Close:** "Share or steal?"
