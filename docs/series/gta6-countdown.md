# "Death waits for GTA 6": series format (8 Nov 2026)

Jonny, 8 Nov: a second GTA 6 pre-launch video in the new style, because the first one ("Not Yet") was the account's first clip with 20+ likes. Then a third pre-launch clip, a launch-day clip and a post-launch clip, so the account has a small series riding a trend everyone is talking about.

**Release date (checked 8 Nov 2026):** GTA 6 launches **Thursday 19 November 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar announced the date in Nov 2025 (after two delays), and the Xbox store page and every tracker still list it today. Pre-load dates differ between sources (10 or 12 Nov), so no episode depends on the pre-load. Recheck the date on each posting day.

Every episode brief in this folder follows this page. When an episode brief and this page disagree, the episode brief wins. If Jonny makes storyboard panels for an episode, his panels win over both (run `qa/panels.mjs`).

They fight, the Wizard holds up a calendar with launch day circled in red, Death parks his scythe and sits down to wait with him. Burned-in caption: "when death comes 18 days before GTA 6".

What worked:
1. **Frame 0 was already the fight.** Death coming through the door, scythe up, the Wizard's shield in the same medium-wide. That is reach rule B1 (troublemaker and victim, trouble already happening), before we had the rule.
2. **A joke everyone already knows, made literal.** "I can't die before GTA 6" is the internet's GTA 6 joke. The day count in the caption made it feel live.
3. **A reversal with a warm ending.** It looks like a death scene and ends with two friends on a sofa waiting for a game.
4. **9 seconds.**

What it lacked:
- **No comment or share trigger** (0 and 0), and an old-style caption ("#GTA #fighting #relatablevideos #cartoon #friends").
- **The wide at 2.8 to 4 s shows an empty room** (both characters out of frame at 3.5 s). The biggest drop is between 1 and 2 s (82% to 58%) and it keeps falling through that empty wide.
- **The GTA name didn't bring extra reach.** Search traffic was 0.1%; 96.7% came from For You, like every other post. The likes came from the clip itself, so the series has to win on the clip, with GTA 6 as the reason to watch now.

## The series idea: Death is waiting for GTA 6 too

Episode 1 ended with Death agreeing to wait. The series keeps that deal running: **Death lives on the friends' sofa until GTA 6 comes out**, scythe parked by the wall, controller in hand. Each episode is one "the friend who…" moment of the GTA 6 countdown that everybody is living right now, and Death's deal is the thread a follower comes back for (when it launches, does he take the Wizard?).

## What stays the same in every episode

**1. The same place.** The evening apartment from episode 1, rebuilt in the new look: slate-blue patterned walls, a night window with city lights, warm lamps, framed pictures and plants, the blue sofa (now patched with tape where the stuffing tore in episode 1), the cracked shelf still cracked. The TV sits just off camera so its glow lights the faces (as in episode 1's last panel). Time of day and mess change per episode; the layout never does, so the room reads as "theirs".

**2. The calendar (series signature, works on mute).** The same wall calendar from episode 1 hangs above the sofa, always in frame at 0.0: a blank grid, **one red circle** (launch day) and a red X on every day that has passed. No numbers or words on it. The empty cells before the circle are the countdown: 7 in episode 2, 1 in episode 3, none on launch day. Someone touches or looks at the calendar in every episode.

**3. The parked scythe.** Death's scythe leans against the wall at the end of the sofa, within his reach, in frame in every episode. When he picks it up, the room knows what it means.

**4. The same friends, each a fixed type.**

| Friend | Type | Always |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaper = Death** | waiting for GTA 6 too | Silent and deadpan, at most one low gibberish line per episode. Hood up. Small, precise moves (character bible). He keeps his deal. Secretly as hyped as anyone (a tiny nod when nobody is looking). |
| **Wizard** | has waited since 2013 | Keeper of the calendar: crosses off the days, holds it up in a crisis. His line is **"NOT YET!"** (the episode 1 title, the series catchphrase, always subtitled the same way). |
| **Phoenix** | too much energy | Hyped beyond reason (his energy meter from the Too Much Energy series: crest and wingtip flames grow with his excitement; his flames never light anything). The troublemaker. |
| **Gunslinger** | never has money | Episodes 3 to 5 only. Pocket pat and moth, "Next time's on me!" (from Never Has Money). |

Never more than four faces on screen.

**5. The same rules for the trend.** We reference GTA 6 through the situation and the caption only.
- **No Rockstar logos, no "VI" lettering, no game characters, art, music, footage or look-alike scenes** (no neon beach city, no pink-and-teal sunset key art). The TV shows only a plain glow, a generic progress bar or a crossed-out Wi-Fi icon. Phones show plain cards ("DELAYED" and a sad face), no outlet or brand names. No game box.
- The words "GTA 6" appear only in Jonny's in-app title and the caption, never in the picture.
- Episode 1 gave the countdown as a burned-in caption. From now on Jonny adds the title with TikTok's text tool (house rule), so the day count is always right on the day he posts.

## Rules every episode follows (from project memory)

- **New look (template, 8 Nov):** `set.cel` + `animeTiming`, lighting 2 ("halfway" shade, pencil strokes, no paper grain, half boil), castLook "sheet", motion natural + feet 2 + hold 2 + overlap/timing/solve, real physics for everything that falls or is thrown, sound guard. Whole frame sharp (no depth of field). Painted back wall plate for the room (the env pipeline is for outdoor places) with real 3D geometry for everything a character touches: sofa, table, calendar, scythe, door, controllers.
- **Every gibberish line subtitled** (classic style, between y 470 and 1360, never over a face). Voices: the current VoxCPM2 voices for Phoenix, Reaper and Wizard; Gunslinger's Never Has Money voice.
- **Conflict:** glares, eye rolls, synced head turns, overlapping gibberish. Nobody is cruel and nobody gets hurt: cartoon impacts only. Death can look ominous as a misdirect, but the reveal is always warm (character bible).
- **Staging:** the subject of the moment owns the front of the frame. Characters a body-width apart unless the beat needs contact.
- **Hands:** 3-finger gloves, grip library holds (phone, thin for the marker, controller held with thumbs on top and fingers under it, as in episode 1).
- **Workflow:** start from `storyboards/templates/clip-template.mjs` (the episode 1 generator `storyboards/trend/not-yet/make-not-yet.mjs` is only a reference for the room). Fable reviews storyboard and cut. Preflight green before any full render. Merge only on Jonny's "merge it" with every check green. No render until Jonny says go.

## MrBeast pass (8 Nov 2026: Jonny "id like to try all of it")

From the playbook (https://claude.ai/artifact/YKf2fEBHz3hGergkmUDtLY). Every episode brief from 03 on ends with its own
"MrBeast pass" section. **That section wins over the brief above it.** Where it also departs from Jonny's panels, the
line is marked ⚑, and Jonny settles those when he gives the go. Nothing is built before his go.

1. **The extreme version.** Each brief lists three versions of its big gag and builds the most extreme one that still
   reads on mute.
2. **Frame 0 shows the title.** Put the in-app title over frame 0. Every word of the situation must be visible in the
   picture, or the title or the opening changes. Each posting card carries a FRAME-0 line saying what shows it.
3. **Frame 0 lighting is optional.** Our test on 34 posts (8 Nov) found no link between opening brightness and the
   0:03 hold, so there is no brightness target. Light frame 0 the way the scene needs. Still keep the troublemaker's
   and the victim's faces readable, and no dark hood against a dark wall at 0.0 (episode 2's readability warning on
   Death).
4. **Stair-step.** Every gag is bigger than the one before, and the biggest comes last.
5. **Start at step 3.** Frame 0 is the disaster already under way, never the build-up.
6. **The twist at the halfway mark** (4.0–6.0 s).
7. **Withhold the result.** The answer to the title's question shows only in the last 1.5 s.
8. **One wow moment** per episode that only our engine and cast can do (physics, a time-lapse, a slow-motion pile).
9. **Abrupt end.** The payoff is the last frame, then the hard cut at 10.0 s. No wind-down.
10. **Cheap.** Every episode reuses the episode 2 apartment, calendar, sofa, scythe and props; new props only where a
    gag needs them.
11. **Best TikTok clip, not the prettiest.** Polish only what lifts the 0:03 hold, the finish rate or shares.
