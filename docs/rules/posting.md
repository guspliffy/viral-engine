# Posting: the post pack and how videos are judged

Claude never posts. The producer posts by hand and labels the video as AI content. Rules from the reach rules v2
(7 Nov 2026), the producer's title notes (9 Nov) and the data reads (11-15 Nov).

## The post pack (on every delivery and every "go", unasked)

Every delivery message, and the reply to the producer's go on a video, ends with one copy-paste block:

```
TITLE (TikTok text tool, <= 6 words, same words as caption line 1): Splitting the bill should be illegal
CAPTION:
Splitting the bill should be illegal 💸
Which one are you, the payer or the "next time's on me"?
#funnyanimation #animation #brokefriend
POLL: Which one are you? / I pay 💳 / I'm the "next time" friend 🙈
PLAYLIST: <series name> · SLOT: 07-09 or 22-24 Berlin
OPENER: A or B · STAKE LINE: "<the first narrator line>"
```

- **Line 1** = the situation in plain, searchable English. The title takes a side the audience can argue with
  ("10x more extreme", "more polarizing"), still the situation, never the payoff. Offer 2 alternates, strongest first.
  Exact numbers beat round ones for counts ("97 photos before the first bite").
- **Line 2** = a self-ID question: "Which one are you, ... or ...?". Never "Who is your friend that..." or "tag him"
  (0 comments on 31 posts with that style).
- **Hashtags**: exactly `#funnyanimation #animation` + one situation tag (#friendgroup, #bestfriends, #brokefriend ...).
  No #comedy, #cartoon, #viral, #fyp. Check the spelling (a Cyrillic "у" slipped in 10 times).
- **Poll**: the question + 2 options matching line 2, pinned right after posting.
- **Playlist** per series; **slot** 07-09 or 22-24 Berlin.
- **Cover** = the video's biggest (10x) frame with no text on it.
- After 1 hour the producer checks TikTok Studio for a "not eligible for For You" notice and the AI label.
- Post every video on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (the same file); a long film goes to YouTube as a
  16:9 video over 3:00 with its thumbnail as a separate upload, and its vertical parts to TikTok.

## The 10-second stranger test (the producer's own step, 15 Nov, on trial)

Before posting, the producer may show the first 10 s to one person at a time, in person or by screen share: never in
a group (people copy each other's reactions), never saying it is a test, and the viewer holds the phone and may pause.
A video handed back after 10 s ("nice") is not ready; a viewer who keeps watching is the goal. When several people
stop or pause at the same second, that second gets one fix (in his study one background colour change stopped the
pausing), then the next person. The producer tells Claude the result (handed back at Xs / kept watching / paused at
Xs) and it goes into the video's `experiments/` note, so the test can be compared with the 24 h numbers.

## How a video is judged (audience data decides, not the producer's score)

- Read each posted video after 24 h, YouTube Shorts at 72 h. Retention decides first: hold at 0:03 and 0:05, finish
  rate, average watch time; on YouTube Shorts "viewed" against "swiped away" is the cleanest hook number. Likes and
  shares per 1,000 views come second (the producer, 18 Nov: retention pushed reach, likes did not). Never decide on
  one post.
- What the data showed so far (trials, not laws): an impossible fact or number at 0.0 opens best; likes follow the
  payoff, not the hook; the strongest formats were the mystery films, There's a catch and Friends try to; music from
  the first frame held better at 0:03; a freeze before a punchline lost a third of the viewers in one second. 19 Nov:
  a ~30 s dialogue sketch in the action at frame 0 with one counter on screen (Court Day: 73 % at 0:03, 49 % finish,
  74 % viewed on YouTube) was the first post past TikTok's ~750-view batch; a life question or a scenic title shot at
  frame 0 lost a quarter of viewers in the first second (26 % viewed).
- The producer tests many formats and hooks on purpose. Do not tell him to stop; propose one change per test,
  recorded in `experiments/`, compared at the same post age.
- Most TikTok viewers are in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and 18-34: hooks must read without English.
