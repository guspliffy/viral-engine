# Too Much Energy, episode 1: the library — Viral Engine prompt (5 Nov 2026)

Jonny picked this on 5 Nov as one of seven episodes of the "That one friend with too much energy" series. Read 00-SERIES-FORMAT.md in this folder first: Phoenix's energy meter, THE GRIN (7.30–8.50), the "ting" logo and the posting pattern come from there. Too Much Energy (clip 23) is the pilot.

It applies the reach playbook (reach-playbook-2026-11-04.md) and Jonny's lab picks: a gibberish yelp in the first half second (subtitled, Jonny's 4 Nov rule), stronger force with 3-frame hit-stops, stronger faces with longer holds. The whole frame stays sharp: no depth of field.

---

Create an **exactly 10-second, 9:16 Potato Brawl animated comedy**, 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps.

**Core joke:** The quietest place on earth and the loudest friend in it. Phoenix whisper-screams and somersaults between the shelves while Reaper and Wizard try to read. Wizard's calming spell only makes him more hyped. He rides the rolling ladder along the shelves like a scooter, it hits the end stop, and the shelves topple like dominoes across the room. He grins. Then the librarian, Reaper and Wizard all shush him at once, and the gust blows out his crest flame like a birthday candle.

**Why it should travel:**
- **Share hook:** everyone has the friend who can't be quiet in a quiet place, and every reader has been shushed by the wrong person.
- **Comment trigger (running joke):** the shush that blows his flame out is new: "did they just turn him off?". Plus the series question.
- **Running joke:** the energy meter and the grin; the snuffed flame can come back in later episodes as the only thing that stops him.
- **Plain search word:** "library".

## Cast

Phoenix, two tired friends and one guest.

- **Phoenix** (too much energy): front of frame, always moving. Holds a big picture book about dragons (a dragon on the cover, no title text) for the first beat.
- **Reaper** (the tired one): seated at the reading table, centre-left behind Phoenix, reading a thick book held up in both hands. Hood up. No scythe.
- **Wizard** (tries to fix it with a spell): seated beside Reaper with a stack of books and his staff leaning on the table.
- **Sorcerer** (guest, the librarian): at the issue desk back-left, his green orb glowing on the desk as a reading lamp. Purple hood, reading glasses perched on the hood's edge if the sheet design allows; otherwise none.

Preserve all approved character designs from the reference sheet. Clean cinematic anime linework, controlled cel shadows, crisp faces and restrained highlights.

## Environment (one painted set)

Try a **layered painted environment** first (new env `old-library-hall`, about 3 minutes). If the coverage check or the look fails indoors, fall back to a painted plate plus 3D room geometry.

- **Far:** a tall old reading hall with arched windows, warm afternoon light falling in dusty beams, a gallery level with more shelves.
- **Mid:** the issue desk (back-left), green-shaded reading lamps, a globe, a cart of returned books.
- **Near:** a row of **five tall shelves on the right, standing side by side across the frame from right to left**, parallel to the camera's view so they tip sideways across the picture (a fall into depth doesn't read; Copy Everything lesson). A brass rail runs along the front shelf at mid height with a **rolling ladder** on it; the rail has a visible end stop at its left end, right beside the last shelf.
- **Real geometry:** the floor, the reading table, the chairs, the five shelves, the ladder and rail, the books that fall (real objects, not a texture), the issue desk.
- **Light:** warm sun beams with dust drifting in them, the lamps' green-gold glow, Phoenix's crest as a small warm light. All sharp.

## Camera and look

- One wide from the front, slightly low, at potato eye height: the table centre-left, the shelves along the right, the issue desk back-left. Phoenix owns the clear floor in front of the table.
- THE GRIN uses the series framing (medium close, front-left, eye level) with the toppled shelves behind him.
- **Hit-stops:** (1) Wizard's spell hitting Phoenix at 3.10, (2) the first shelf tipping at 6.20.
- **Space for the title:** the top 20% clear from 0 to 2 s. Don't burn in a title.

## Sound

- **Music:** tiptoe pizzicato strings with a celesta, about 132 bpm in D minor, `lowCut` around 150 Hz. It speeds up a little with each meter step and drops out at the first shelf.
- **Effects:** book pages flapping, his feet thumping on the wooden floor, a soft `ai/flare` on each meter step, the spell sparkle (`charge`, short), the ladder's wheels rumbling along the rail (`doorslide`), a metal clang at the end stop (`bell-heavy`, short), five wooden shelf thuds in a row (`kenney/impact-plank-medium`), books cascading (`thud-soft` layered), dust puffs, the "ting" at 7.80, the three-voice `shhh`, the flame going out (`ai/snuff`).
- **Voices (all subtitled):** Phoenix's whisper-scream yelp at 0.05, "Psst! PSST! Dragons!" (lower third, y about 1240). Wizard 2.6: "Calm… down…" while casting. Sorcerer 3.5: "SHH!". Phoenix 4.2: "Wheee!" (whispered, wide mouth). At 8.90 Reaper, Wizard and Sorcerer together: "SHHHHH!" (one subtitle). Reaper says nothing else.

## Exact seven-beat timing

1. **0.00–1.50 — ALREADY TOO MUCH.** Wide. Frame 0: Phoenix mid-hop in front of the table, holding the dragon book open toward Reaper, whisper-screaming with his mouth wide and his wings fluttering. Meter small. Reaper and Wizard read, perfectly still. The Sorcerer looks up over his orb. The shelves and the ladder are already in view on the right.

2. **1.50–3.30 — SOMERSAULT, THEN THE SPELL.** Phoenix tosses the book onto the table (it lands open in front of Reaper, thud) and does two somersaults down the aisle and back, landing in a pose. Meter up one size. Reaper slowly lifts his own book higher until it covers his face. Wizard sighs, points his staff and casts a soft blue "calm" sparkle at Phoenix (2.6). At 3.10 the sparkle hits him (hit-stop) and his meter jumps **two** sizes instead of going down. Wizard stares at his staff.

3. **3.30–5.30 — THE LADDER.** The Sorcerer stands up behind his desk: "SHH!" (3.5), and Phoenix reads it as a wave and waves back. He jumps onto the rolling ladder, kicks off, and rides it along the rail across the front of the shelves, wings fanning for speed, books on the shelves shaking as he passes. Reaper lowers his book a little to watch with a long, slow blink.

4. **5.30–7.30 — THE BREAK.** At 5.90 the ladder hits the end stop (clang). Phoenix flies off it in a somersault and lands in the front of frame. The jolt tips the last shelf, and at 6.20 (hit-stop) it falls sideways into the next one: the five shelves go down one after another from left to right across the picture, books spilling in arcs and dust rising. The music drops out. The last shelf comes to rest leaning on the wall. Nobody is under any shelf: they fall away from the table and the desk.

5. **7.30–8.50 — THE GRIN.** The series block: medium close on Phoenix, the toppled shelves and the dust behind him. Head turn to us at 7.45, grin and pose at 7.70, "ting" and a small crest flare at 7.80, hold until 8.50.

6. **8.50–9.40 — THE SHUSH.** Back to the wide. Reaper, Wizard and the Sorcerer stand side by side in the book rubble, take one breath together, and at 8.90 shush him with one giant "SHHHHH!" (each with one glove finger to the lips). The gust blows across the room: pages flutter, Phoenix's wings blow back, and **his crest flame goes out like a birthday candle** (`ai/snuff`, 9.10). The meter is at zero for the first time in the series.

7. **9.40–10.00 — SMOKE.** A thin curl of smoke rises from his crest. He blinks, still grinning a little. **Hard cut at exactly 10.00.**

## Continuity and contact rules

- One dragon book: in his hands until 1.6, then on the table in front of Reaper for the rest of the clip.
- The ladder stays on its rail; only its wheels move. Phoenix's gloves hold the ladder's side rails with no gaps.
- The shelves fall in order, each one hitting the next; the books come only from the shelves that fall.
- Phoenix's flames never touch a book or a shelf. At 9.10 the crest flame shrinks to nothing; the wing-tip flames go out with it and come back only after the clip.
- Nobody is hit by a shelf or a book.
