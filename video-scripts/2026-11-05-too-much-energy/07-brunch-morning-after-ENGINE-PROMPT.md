Create an **exactly 10-second, 9:16 Potato Brawl animated comedy**, 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps.

**Core joke:** Sunday brunch on a sunny café terrace after a long night. Reaper, Wizard and Viking slump under the parasol's shade in sunglasses, barely alive. Phoenix has four empty espresso cups stacked in front of him and orders a fifth. He downs it, then steals Reaper's too, and the meter maxes out: he shoots straight up through the parasol like a rocket, leaving a Phoenix-shaped hole. He grins from above. Now a beam of sunlight shines through the hole straight onto the three wrecked friends, and they hiss and slide under the table like vampires.

**Why it should travel:**
- **Share hook:** every friend group has one person who is fine the morning after, and it's always the worst person to be fine.
- **Comment trigger (pick a side):** "Which one are you at brunch?" (the sunglasses three or him). This replaces the series question.
- **Running joke:** the meter (here it's literally fed, one espresso per size) and the grin.
- **Plain search word:** "brunch".

## Exact seven-beat timing

1. **0.00–1.50 — ALREADY TOO MUCH.** Wide. Frame 0: Phoenix bouncing on his chair, waving both arms at the waiter, "One more! ONE MORE!". The tower of four empty espresso cups wobbles with each bounce. Meter small. The three friends in sunglasses slump in the shade, not moving. The waiter is already walking over with the fifth espresso.

2. **1.50–3.30 — CUP FIVE.** The waiter sets the espresso down; Phoenix downs it in one gulp and slams the cup on top of the tower. Meter up one size. He starts to vibrate: the table rattles, the cups chatter, and Viking's head slides off his fist and bonks onto the table. Wizard: "Too loud…" without lifting his head. The waiter backs away to the door.

3. **3.30–5.30 — REAPER'S CUP.** Phoenix spots Reaper's big coffee, "You done?", takes it out of his gloves and downs it too (hit-stop on the gulp at 4.30). Meter at its biggest. Reaper's gloves close on nothing; his sunglasses slide slowly down his nose and he glares over them. Phoenix's chair hops off the ground with each vibration.

4. **5.30–7.30 — THE BREAK.** He can't sit any more: he launches straight up like a rocket, wings blazing, and tears through the parasol (hit-stop at 6.00), leaving a perfect Phoenix-shaped hole with ragged edges. Shreds of cream cloth flutter down. The music cuts out. He hovers above the parasol.

5. **7.30–8.50 — THE GRIN.** The series block at hover height: medium close on Phoenix floating above the torn parasol, the hole below him. Head turn, grin and pose, "ting" at 7.80, hold until 8.50 (`sound.flaps: false` for the hover).

6. **8.50–9.50 — THE BEAM.** Back to the wide. A sharp beam of bright sun now shines through the hole straight onto the table and the three friends. They flinch, hiss like vampires ("Hsss!"), and all three slide slowly down off their chairs and under the table into the shade, in sync.

7. **9.50–10.00 — BREAKFAST?** Phoenix drops softly back onto his chair, looks at the three empty chairs: "…Breakfast?". **Hard cut at exactly 10.00.**
