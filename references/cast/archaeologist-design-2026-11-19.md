# The Archaeologist

## 1. Concept

She is a field archaeologist with a compact, low-set pear silhouette, a dusty violet field jacket and a wide plum field hat. A single leaf tilts out of the crown. She keeps a small conservation brush at her side until she can point to one thing the viewer can see, such as a surface on a granite box at Saqqara or a bed of rock at the Sphinx. She listens still, lifts one eyebrow and delivers the evidence with a trace of amusement. The low hat crown, spare workwear and measured gestures give her the bearing of someone used to working on site.

Use the same low pear taper in profile.
- Instance Spud's skin material and texture, eyes and hands with their original colours and asset scale. Set the eye pair at `0.75H`, centred across the face. Sleeves meet the stock hands at the wrist rig joints.
- Use one stock Spud leaf with its tip `0.09H` above the crown. Set its root `0.06W` toward her left of centre and tilt it `32°` from vertical toward her anatomical left. The leaf grows from her scalp through a short painted slit in the crown.

## 4. Hand prop

One conservation brush, `0.23H` long. Use a slim tapered cylinder for the dark walnut handle `#513B34`, a short cylinder for the dull silver ferrule `#A7A19A` and a flattened rounded cylinder for the pale tan bristles `#D5BA91`. Give the bristle tip `0.055H` of length and `0.035W` of width. Paint two uneven hair strokes near the tip. Grip the handle at its midpoint in her anatomical right hand. At rest, hold the hand just clear of the right hip with the brush pointing `25°` forward from straight down. To point, lift the forearm until the brush is near horizontal and roll the wrist to aim the bristles. At the Sphinx, let the tip follow the line of a bed or stop beside a block joint. Her left hand stays available and relaxed.

## 7. Face and debate expressions

Use `E` for the diameter of a stock Spud eye. Keep the stock eye shapes and pupil scale, and let the brow poses carry the acting. Lift values are measured at the brow midpoint from the default.

## 8. TTS voice

Woman in her early 40s, speaking fluent English with a light Cairene Egyptian accent and a warm mid-low register.  
About 145 words per minute, with a short pause before the evidence. Calm, dry, gently amused, with emphasis on the concrete detail.

## 9. Risks

- A domed crown or curled brim can turn into a costume gag. Keep the crown `0.10H` high and the brim flat in profile.
- A blue or black lighting shift can erase the jacket’s violet and the hat’s edge. Keep violet in the filled shadows and check the dark edge over sand and the lilac bevel over sky.
- A high brow arch, a held grin or fast brush flourishes can read as smug. Keep brow movements small and settle the brush at her hip between evidence beats.
